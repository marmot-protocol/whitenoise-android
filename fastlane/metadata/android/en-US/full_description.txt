White Noise is a private messenger built on the open Marmot Protocol and Nostr.

Direct messages and group conversations are protected with Messaging Layer Security (MLS), including forward secrecy and post-compromise security. Your private key stays on your device, and White Noise does not require a phone number or email address.

White Noise is non-custodial and works with independent Nostr relays and Blossom media servers. Encrypted conversation media is encrypted before it is uploaded.

If you enable push notifications, White Noise uses Transponder to route encrypted notification events. Anonymous usage and crash reporting are opt-in and can be changed in the app settings.

Privacy policy: https://www.whitenoise.chat/privacy

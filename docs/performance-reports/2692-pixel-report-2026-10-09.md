## 8 October: repeated resource measurements

The older report retains five nominal 60-second samples for each of four
scenarios: all background delivery disabled, Push idle, Local idle and Push
burst. The trace-visible spans were 59.853–60.097 seconds, temperature was
33.5–33.9°C, and no trace data loss was reported. The report failed its resource
campaign screening and did not establish tracker completion.

| Scenario | Samples | Median fixture CPU, ms | Median system CPU rails, J | Median system network category, J | Median system memory category, J |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Disabled idle | 5 | 277.837 | 4.456643 | 7.017905 | 3.118084 |
| Push idle | 5 | 481.310 | 4.839017 | 7.267722 | 3.234269 |
| Local idle | 5 | 369.461 | 4.645503 | 7.056012 | 3.111966 |
| Push burst | 5 | 3672.232 | 5.259831 | 7.288906 | 3.287800 |

Disabled-idle CPU energy range was 244.05% of its median, network 48.55%, and
memory 72.93%. Push-burst CPU energy range was 41.20%. These fail the 25%
screening bound; they are not controlled evidence of app-only battery cost.
The older report's rail grouping includes AOC in its network category; the quick
report below uses modem, radio frontend and Wi-Fi/BT. The tables therefore must
not be used for before/after savings comparisons.

Push posting counts were **5, 5, 5, 5, 0** across the five rounds. Four completed
push-lock trace spans were approximately 3.029, 3.661, 2.418 and 2.923 seconds.
Endpoint BatteryStats service/wake-lock deltas and unchanged scheduled-job
records are retained in the JSON; endpoint intervals include setup and are not
exact trace-window service ownership. Local service deltas were 70.849–79.349
seconds. Zero endpoint deltas do not prove the absence of all background work.

At the intermediate 36-body stage, after a foreground reopen, all 36 expected
distinct synthetic bodies were found in the canonical timeline; the bounded scan
observed zero duplicate IDs. The
five bodies from the unsuccessful fifth Push round were each present once.
Raw database counts were unavailable. Persistence after reopen does not repair
the missing in-window notification evidence. The reconnect attempt produced no
usable trace or owned collector PID; connectivity cleanup was verified.

The subsequent final readback covered **44/44** distinct bodies with zero distinct
duplicate IDs, including the five queued reconnect bodies and three additional
USB functional-test bodies. Both stages reached the oldest fixture seed; neither
is a raw database count. The original report records USB-powered force-stop and
explicit-reopen checks in both modes and a Push permission-restoration check,
each with a fresh post within 60 seconds. They qualify those functional checks,
without qualifying unplugged energy, ordinary process death, Doze, or truthful
permission-loss UI/suppression. The intermediate and final observations are
separate entries in the retained summary JSON.

## 9 October: collector repair and rejected full campaigns

On stock Android 17, the original launch with file-backed config/diagnostic file
descriptors failed with an empty TraceConfig and a SELinux read denial. Identical
configuration through pipes produced parseable five-second charging smoke
traces; malformed configuration retained nonempty rejection diagnostics. This
establishes the collector fix, not a runtime battery optimization.

After review, diagnostics reservation was made atomic with `noclobber`, and
`tee -a` preserves rather than truncates the reserved log. Eight focused launcher
regressions pass, including a log created between path validation and launch.
The revised helper was also sourced on the actual Android mksh R59 shell in a
charging, system-only smoke: a nominal three-second trace parsed with a
2.150443-second visible span. No app/profile settings changed. The tested helper
SHA-256 is `58770573475d140e445843d4fddab53a89125e4b3bdb6d2bbaf0aada77966d97`.
This separately verifies the reviewed launcher, without retesting the battery or
delivery campaign on the later PR revision.

A charging Local functional check posted all five fresh bodies within about
35 seconds and produced a parseable 60-second trace. Its display was ON for
about 16.80 seconds and OFF for 43.10 seconds. It cannot qualify a screen-off
unplugged energy campaign.

The first unplugged full campaign stopped before its first trace when unread
state changed the Settings accessibility label. The replacement passed Local
and Push UI preflights, but its first post-unplug before-snapshot showed an ON
display and Awake wakefulness. The guard rejected setup: **0/25 planned windows
completed**, and later windows never started. These were fixture setup failures;
no production battery root cause was established.

Five sender bursts each recorded five native Sent acknowledgements. After a
receiver cold reopen, all **25/25** fresh bodies had observed canonical rows,
with zero duplicate canonical rows observed in the bounded UI scan. This proves
post-restart persistence for those bodies, without proving posting timing, FCM
transport or global database duplicate absence.

## 9 October: short proof and five captured quick scenarios

The short proof retained a parseable **19.861259-second** trace with approximately
103.023 ms of fixture UID CPU and 24 sampled hardware rails. Actual charging flags
were false at both endpoints, with no simulated battery state. No trace quality
errors were reported. Continuous display-state samples were absent, so endpoint
screen-off and zero display energy do not prove continuous screen-off operation.

The subsequent quick bundle retained **five completed, parseable traces**, one
for each required resource scenario. The additional Local burst failed before
mode selection/capture: the guarded UI driver could not find its Notifications
navigation target after the Settings tap. Its raw diagnostics are retained;

Standalone Perfetto SQL was executed against these actual traces, using trace

suspend state and bounded phase events. All five have charging flags false at
both endpoints and no reported trace quality errors. Energy is system-wide:
fixture observers, tracing, other system work and radio activity remain included.
No app-exclusive mAh, battery-life improvement or dominant avoidable cost is
established.

The Disabled trace has no fixture entry in the joined CPU-cycle query. That
query does not establish continuous process presence; endpoint process guards
passed. Its CPU measurement remains **unknown**, not zero. The three
idle traces have no continuous display-state coverage. Reconnect deliberately
woke the display to restore connectivity, with approximately 10.527 seconds ON;
it is a diagnostic reconnect sample. Push burst includes approximately
0.689 seconds ON. Suspend analysis reports the device awake throughout these
short windows; these are not a natural Doze qualification. There is only one
sample per scenario, without reviewed budgets, balanced rounds or a matched
baseline. Wake-lock lifetime, exact service ownership and complete recovery
attempt counts remain unknown in the quick report.

The Local persistent stream service was present at both idle endpoints; it was
absent at Push and Disabled idle endpoints. Reconnect retained one recovery
attempt phase, network-restoration and catch-up readiness markers. Endpoint
presence and phase counts do not establish service lifetimes or all attempts.

### Quick delivery observations

Both sender bursts recorded **5/5 native Sent acknowledgements**. All five Push
submissions occurred within the planned 60-second window, about 16.98–45.30
seconds after its planned start. Receiver observations inside the actual Push
trace covered only **3/5** fresh bodies. Their submission-to-first-observed-post
latencies were **9.280, 1.164 and 1.404 seconds**. The bounded phase export contains
one real high-priority FCM callback marker. This does not prove all five messages
arrived via FCM or posted within the window.

The observer verified clock consistency and filtered by disposable profile,
package, fresh synthetic bodies and posting time before serializing anonymous
counts/timestamps. Callback observations measure Android posting as seen by the
fixture listener, rather than native ingestion latency. Zero multiple-card-key
observations among the observed bodies do not prove global notification deduplication.

No Local burst trace or timed posting receipt exists. Following a fixture cold
reopen, **10/10** fresh bodies across both quick bursts had canonical rows, with
zero duplicate canonical rows observed in the bounded scan. Later persistence
does not establish whether the two unobserved Push posts were delayed, missing
or outside the observer's coverage.

uninstalled. Owner was selected, unlocked by the user, and the restored app's

without inspecting conversation content. Campaign helper processes ended, and
only the verified owned sender emulator was stopped, retaining its AVD data.
Backups, original APKs and recovery artifacts remain private and preserved.

## Acceptance assessment

The source tracker requires every native child to be complete plus guarded
exact-head coverage of both modes, transition rollback, process restoration,
Optimized/Unrestricted policy, capability and permission loss, privacy-safe phase
export and agreed idle/push/reconnect/keep-connected energy budgets. This report
does not replace the authoritative native sub-issue hierarchy with a checklist.

| Required evidence | Current result |
| --- | --- |

| Wake-lock/service/job/reconnect/radio attribution | Partial older endpoints/spans and new phase markers; exact quick ownership remains unknown |
| Reviewed budgets and dominant avoidable cost | Not established; no matched before/after optimization result |
| Correct timed delivery in both modes | Partial Push timing, older charging Local functional evidence; complete guarded timing matrix absent |
| Rollback, process restoration, permission/capability recovery | Full exact-head physical matrix not established |
| Optimized and Unrestricted policy coverage | Optimized only in these runs |
| Guarded GrapheneOS regression matrix | Unavailable; only stock Pixel supplied |
| Every required native child complete | Battery child remains open; this report cannot close the tracker |

Collector tests and green CI qualify their specific source/tooling revision;
they do not satisfy the device gates above. Keep the tracker and child open,
without a `Closes` directive, until the explicit acceptance evidence is complete.

[ ] **SET-060 — Settings grows from the account avatar and returns into it** — With one signed-in account, tap the chat-list avatar to open Settings, then press Back (top-bar arrow and system Back). Add a second account and repeat through the avatar's account selector and its Settings button. Repeat in light, dark and AMOLED themes, in RTL, at the largest font size, in the two-pane layout and after rotating while Settings is open. Reverse mid-open and mid-close, and set Developer options → Animator duration scale to Off. Record the transition with a screen recorder and step through it frame by frame. → **Expected:** Opening is one rounded container that starts as the avatar circle and grows to fill the screen while its corners square off; the Chats area behind it dims, and the Settings rows fade in at their final positions during the later part of the open, never scaled or moved. Back contracts the same single container into the avatar, which reappears as the container lands. Nothing splits into separate boxes, fans out from a point or flies toward the New message button. AMOLED shows a thin outline on the moving container; light and dark move the same way without it. The account selector's Settings button uses the active avatar as the origin. Reversing mid-flight continues from the drawn frame without a jump. With animations off, both directions switch at once. After rotation or a pane change, Back still lands on the avatar or settles without motion; it never animates toward a stale position. Chats with screenshots blocked stay protected: no Chats image appears during the transition.

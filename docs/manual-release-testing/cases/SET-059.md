1. [ ] **SET-059 — Linked devices roster** — This needs a linked-devices test build and a disposable account with at least one other linked device; ordinary builds have no Linked devices row. Open Settings, Account, Linked devices. Hold a device row. Rename or remove a device from the other device and watch the list. Turn on airplane mode, leave and reopen the screen, tap Retry, then turn airplane mode off and tap Retry again. Switch profiles from the account switcher and return. Repeat in dark, AMOLED, RTL and at the largest font size, and with TalkBack. → **Expected:** This device is listed first under its own heading, other devices follow under Other devices, and a device whose removal has not finished is under Being removed. Each row shows the device's name (or Unnamed device), its app and platform, the date it was linked and, for other devices, Last active with a date and time or No activity seen yet. Nothing says a device is online. A device marked inactive, signing out or moved says so. The list updates by itself when another device changes it. A failed load says the list couldn't be loaded and keeps the rows already shown with a Retry row; it never shows an empty list instead. After switching profiles the list is the new profile's, never the previous one's. Holding a row explains what Last active means. Text wraps without clipping and TalkBack reads each row as one item.

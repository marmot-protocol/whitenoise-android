[ ] **MED-037 — Downloads never open what they fetch** — With every automatic-download cell off, receive an image, an album of photos and a video, a voice note, a PDF, a plain-text file, a valid APK and a file in the Shared media Files tab. Tap each one's Download action, or the attachment itself, once; rotate the phone and send the app to the background and back while one download runs. Then tap each downloaded attachment again, and with TalkBack enabled focus an image while it downloads. → **Expected:** Each first tap only downloads: progress shows in place and, when the download finishes, the image, video poster, voice play button or file card simply becomes available, with no viewer, reader, external app, installer or playback starting by itself, including after rotation or the round trip. The second tap opens the viewer, reader, external app or installer, or starts playback, exactly once. A downloading image announces Downloading with no Open action; an image already on the device that is still being prepared announces Opening with an Open action. No control on an attachment that is not on the device reads or announces Play or Open: a video or voice note shows Download until its download is accepted and a progress ring announced as Downloading until it lands, including a Shared media voice row, which offers Download rather than Play. A Shared media file or voice row that is not on the device downloads on its first tap and opens or plays on the next; the reader's own voice note that is still uploading plays on its first tap.

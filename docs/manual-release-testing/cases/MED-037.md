[ ] **MED-037 — Downloads never open what they fetch** — With every automatic-download cell off, receive an image, an album of photos and a video, a voice note, a PDF, a plain-text file, a valid APK and a file in the Shared media Files tab. Tap each one's Download action, or the attachment itself, once; rotate the phone and send the app to the background and back while one download runs. Then tap each downloaded attachment again, and with TalkBack enabled focus an image while it downloads. → **Expected:** Each first tap only downloads: progress shows in place and, when the download finishes, the image, video poster, voice play button or file card simply becomes available, with no viewer, reader, external app, installer or playback starting by itself, including after rotation or the round trip. The second tap opens the viewer, reader, external app or installer, or starts playback, exactly once. A downloading image announces Downloading with no Open action; an image already on the device that is still being prepared announces Opening with an Open action. A Shared media file or voice row that is not on the device downloads on its first tap and opens or plays on the next.

[ ] **SYS-015 — Chat links** — With two signed-in accounts, open `marmot://chat/<group-id>` and the flavor-specific `<scheme>://chat/<group-id>` from another app for a chat held only by the inactive account, then for one held by the active account. Repeat from a cold start, a warm start and while another chat is open. Join one group with both accounts, make a third account active, and open its link; choose an account, then repeat and cancel. Open links with an odd-length, non-hex, extra-segment and unknown group ID. With App Lock enabled and the lock showing, open a valid and an unknown link. → **Expected:** A valid link opens that chat once, switching to the account that holds it; the active account wins when it holds the chat. A chat held by several inactive accounts asks "Open chat in" with each account's name, opens the chosen one, and Cancel leaves the current screen unchanged. Malformed IDs show "That chat link isn't valid" and unknown IDs show "Chat not found"; neither opens or switches to any chat. Under App Lock nothing opens and no message appears until unlock, after which the link is handled once. Rotating or recreating the app afterwards does not reopen the link. Profile links still open profiles.

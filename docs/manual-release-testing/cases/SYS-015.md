[ ] **SYS-015 — Chat links** — With two signed-in accounts, open `marmot://chat/<group-id>` and the flavor-specific `<scheme>://chat/<group-id>` from another app for a chat held only by the inactive account, then for one held by the active account. Repeat from a cold start, a warm start and while another chat is open. Join one group with both accounts, make a third account active, and open its link; rotate the chooser, choose an account, then repeat and cancel. In a group's details, tap Advanced → Copy chat link, paste it into a message in another chat and send it; tap it, then send and tap a link to an unknown chat, a malformed chat link and a profile link. Check that a DM and a pending invite show no Copy chat link row. Open links with an odd-length, non-hex, extra-segment and unknown group ID. With App Lock enabled and the lock showing, open a valid and an unknown link. Malformed IDs show "That chat link isn't valid" and unknown IDs show "Chat not found"; neither opens or switches to any chat. Under App Lock nothing opens and no message appears until unlock, after which the link is handled once. Rotating or recreating the app afterwards does not reopen the link. Profile links still open profiles.

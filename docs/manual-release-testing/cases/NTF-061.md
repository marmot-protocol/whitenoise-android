1. [ ] **NTF-061 — Opening the app while it starts in the background** — With Push notifications selected, sign in to one profile and send White Noise to the background. Kill only its process, for example with `adb shell am kill` and the app's package name, and confirm the process is gone. Do not force-stop it: Android does not start a force-stopped app for a push. Have a contact send a message so the push starts the app in the background, and open it from the launcher at once, before the chat list appears. Repeat at least ten times, including opening it from the new message's notification. → **Expected:** The signed-in profile and its chats open every time. The welcome or sign-in screen never appears while the profile is still signed in, and notifications keep arriving afterwards.

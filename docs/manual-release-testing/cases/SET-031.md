1. [ ] **SET-031 — Quarantined group recovery** — With developer mode off, confirm Quarantined groups is absent from Developer Tools; enable developer mode and open it. Check loading, a confirmed empty inventory, a failed load with Refresh, and a prepared quarantined group for each supported coarse reason. Verify only a shortened group ID is shown. Retry a group that can recover, one that remains quarantined and a failing retry; prepare a group quarantined for a failed rewind restore with the background connection on, swipe White Noise away from recents and reopen it, then force stop it in Android settings and reopen it; tap repeatedly and refresh during recovery. Leave during recovery and reopen immediately, then switch accounts and replace the runtime while loading or retrying, including switching A → B → A. Disable developer mode while the destination is restored, and repeat in dark theme, RTL and large text. With developer mode off, prepare a rewind-restore quarantine on the active account, on a background signed-in account, and once before the app opens its event stream; then force stop and reopen. → **Expected:** The Developer Tools row reports loading/error/count for the current account, leaves loading when the account becomes unavailable, and reloads when that account becomes eligible again. A successful recovery removes its row and the group returns through the normal chat projection; a failed subsequent list refresh preserves the recovery outcome. Unsuccessful recovery leaves history intact and never bypasses validation or rejoins a group. A failed rewind restore shows its shortened group ID, guidance to force stop White Noise or restart the phone, and no Retry. Swiping the app away keeps the process and account open, so the group stays listed; after the force stop the account reopens and the group leaves the list once MDK restores it. Repeated taps on one screen do not overlap retries; MDK owns recovery across screen re-entry. A recovery already admitted may complete after navigation or account/runtime changes, but its stale result is not displayed on the new screen. Unsupported future binding reasons produce a sanitized load failure. Refreshes coalesce, leaving a screen shows no failure, unexpected native read cancellation shows a sanitized load error, private native details are never displayed, and disabling developer mode retires the destination. With developer mode off, each rewind-restore failure shows a "Restart White Noise" notice, once per account and chat in a run, that explains how to force stop or restart the phone, never names raw engine details, and does not return after a force stop unless the failure does.

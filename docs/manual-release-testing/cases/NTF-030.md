1. [ ] **NTF-030 — Notification preview privacy** — In Settings → Notifications, choose Hidden under Notification previews ("previews off" below; Name and message is "on") with app lock disabled. Receive direct/group messages, mentions, reactions, invites and agent activity for two accounts; inspect collapsed/expanded shade, heads-up, lockscreen and grouped cards. Toggle off while a rich card and delayed avatar/name enrichment are pending, then on again before they finish. Relaunch with previews off and tap each generic card. With previews back on, relaunch and receive another message, refresh its sender nickname, and try direct reply; visible rich cards retain history and actions while previously hidden cards stay hidden. Repeat with app lock on, muted/custom conversation channels and important-conversation DND exceptions, both distributions, light/dark/AMOLED themes, RTL, 200% font and TalkBack. With a paired device that normally mirrors Android notifications, send a new private message while Name and message is selected. Select Name only, send a fresh message, reply from its Name-only card, and check the card on both devices. → **Expected:** The default remains on for existing users. Off persists for every account and all user-event cards show only generic app text, without names, avatars, message/history/attachment content or reply/reaction actions. Conversation shortcuts and Android channel names/descriptions use generic labels without deleting existing channels or changing their alert settings. A first message in a new conversation with previews off still uses a generic conversation shortcut. Existing cards become generic silently; late work cannot restore content and turning on alone never reveals old messages. Tap still opens the captured account/chat through app authentication. Custom sounds, vibration, mute, conversation DND exceptions and notification delivery remain unchanged. Open the Android share sheet after the chat list refreshes: owned conversation shortcut labels and icons stay generic while previews are off. TalkBack reads the three preview choices as one group of correctly labelled radio buttons with the saved choice selected. Failures show Retry and block previews in this process; a failed save may leave the previous saved choice after restarting, so retry before restarting. A successfully saved off choice resumes cleanup after restart. The new full-content message preview stays on the originating device. A notification repost containing outgoing reply history is local-only, including when the original card was Name only.

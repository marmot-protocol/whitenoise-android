1. [ ] **MSG-011 — Partial forward failure** — Make one selected target unavailable and forward to it plus a valid target. Then forward a small text attachment and cut the network right after its upload starts, leave it cut for over a minute, and reconnect. Also forward an album of several large photos while other large attachments are downloading on a slow but working connection. → **Expected:** Per-target progress/result distinguishes success and failure; Retry affects only failed targets and does not duplicate successes. The cut upload may fail and go back to in progress by itself up to three times as automatic retries, then stays failed with Retry within about two minutes of the cut, and Retry after reconnecting delivers the attachment exactly once. The album keeps uploading while it makes progress, however slowly, and is delivered once. Only an upload that has not started after about a minute fails with Retry, shows “Upload didn’t start. Retry, or dismiss to stop forwarding.” rather than the generic upload failure, and does not go back to in progress by itself. Hold a forward in each phase for over 15 seconds (stall the source download, delay the upload, and delay the send with relays blocked after upload). → **Expected:** Preparation reads “Getting the attachments is taking longer than usual”, upload reads “Uploading is taking longer than usual”, and sending reads “Sending is taking longer than usual. It can’t be cancelled now and won’t be sent twice.”, each keeping its spinner and the full text readable at large font/RTL. The notice clears when the phase moves on, never offers Cancel while sending, and the delayed send is delivered exactly once when it completes. Hold a forward's destination publish past 30 s (for example by keeping the destination account's engine busy) and confirm the destination fails with “Sending didn’t finish. Retry, or dismiss to stop forwarding. A retry won’t send it twice.”, then Retry. → **Expected:** The retry finishes the message that was waiting, already admitted messages are not sent again, and the destination shows each forwarded message exactly once.

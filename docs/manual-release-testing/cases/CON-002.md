1. [ ] **CON-002 — Transcript chronology** — Inspect a long transcript with date boundaries and multiple senders. Also open a conversation holding more than 50 unread messages, more than the 50 rows MarmotKit loads first. Repeat on a settled page without pending sends, then reopen a page containing both an agent stream start and its completed response. → **Expected:** Messages are chronological, date/unread separators are intelligible, consecutive-message grouping is correct, and latest content is reachable. Check inline date labels, the pinned date pill, full-width unread line and actual unread count in light/dark/AMOLED. Adjacent dates and membership events retain consistent spacing without changing message order or scroll position. The first unread message keeps one interval below the unread line rather than the divider's inset plus a sender-cluster gap. A conversation with unread messages opens at its first unread message rather than the newest one, even when that message lies beyond the first 50 loaded rows; the unread line's count equals the real number of unread messages, which MarmotKit does not cap; and opening alone does not mark anything read. Open the keyboard from the composer and dismiss it with Back: the transcript stays glued to the keyboard for the whole animation in both directions, with no jump when it settles, and the newest message stays against the composer throughout. In a bubble whose body runs to more than one block, a paragraph followed by a link, the timestamp and delivery glyph sit on the last line rather than mid-bubble. A message still sending shows a pending clock rather than a spinner, at the same size as the delivery tick beside it. Native row order remains unchanged on the settled page; a completed response never restarts a live stream or creates a duplicate bubble. Scroll so an unread message passes beneath the composer without clearing it: that message stays unread and keeps its place in the count until it rises clear of the composer, and the same holds for the row behind the conversation's title bar. Rows reach the composer at full strength and then thin away behind it to nothing by the bottom of the screen, and dissolve into the bar above rather than being sliced off mid-bubble; confirm the fade in light, dark and AMOLED, where a covering scrim would show as a grey band. A message whose sender clock is beyond any displayable date (after the year 9999) shows no date label of its own, sits under a separator from its neighbours, and the conversation still opens and scrolls; ordinary neighbours keep their usual labels.

1. [ ] **SET-064 — Manage a linked device** — This needs one disposable account linked on devices A, B and C. On A open Settings, Account, Linked devices and tap device B. Rename it, then rename it to nothing, then try a name longer than 64 bytes. Turn Add to all my chats, Bring chat history and Keep while inactive off and on one at a time, quickly, and watch B's page on C. Tap Remove device, cancel, then remove it. Open this device's own page. Repeat a change while A is offline. → **Expected:** B's page shows its name, app, platform, link date and last activity, a Rename row, three switches and Remove device. The new name appears on A, B and C after a moment; an empty name shows Unnamed device; a too-long name says so and cannot be saved. Each switch changes only its own setting and the others keep their values on every device. A switch moves when the change is applied, never before. If the devices are still applying an earlier change, a notice says to try again in a moment and nothing else changes. Remove device asks for confirmation, and cancelling removes nothing. After removal, B is listed under Being removed until the removal is done, its page says Removal in progress and cannot be edited, and B itself is signed out. This device's own page has no Remove device. Linked devices shows the number of chats not shared with linked devices or still including a removed device only when there are any.

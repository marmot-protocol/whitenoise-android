1. [ ] **CHL-006 — Pin/unpin** — Long-press a chat, pin it, then unpin it. Scroll well below the top of a long list and pin a visible unpinned chat by swipe, by its row menu and by a single-chat selection, with zero, one and several existing pins and with system animations on and off. Repeat once with an injected pin failure, once by changing folder or search before the pin commits, and once by dragging the list while it commits. → **Expected:** Pinned state and ordering update consistently and survive relaunch. Each successful pin returns the list once to its very top (first item, no offset) with the new pin visible in the pinned block; a deep scroll animates only the final rows, and with animations off it settles without motion. A failed pin, an unpin, a remote pin change or a manual pinned-order move leaves the viewport where it was. A folder/search/account change or a new drag before the follow retires it, and the replacement view does not jump. The row context menu is anchored with 8dp spacing and highlights its row without selecting it. Hold until commands appear, then drag through adjacent rows before release: the original native range gesture remains active and no conversation opens. Stationary release transfers focus to the popup. Close and reopen the same row quickly: old command, dismissal and pointer callbacks must not affect the replacement opening. Repeat with TalkBack long-click, Back, and outside dismissal; scrolling the anchor out of composition or leaving Chats dismisses its menu.

1. [ ] **CHL-006 — Pin/unpin** — Long-press a chat, pin it, then unpin it. Scroll well below the top of a long list and pin a visible unpinned chat by swipe, by its row menu and by a single-chat selection, with zero, one and several existing pins and with system animations on and off. Repeat once with an injected pin failure, once by changing folder or search before the pin commits, once by switching folder while the list is still scrolling to the top (the new folder keeps its own position), and once by dragging the list while it commits. Press the left, center and right of the same chat row near both ends of the viewport. Repeat in RTL at large text and open the menu once through TalkBack. → **Expected:** Pinned state and ordering update consistently and survive relaunch. Each successful pin returns the list once to its very top (first item, no offset) with the new pin visible in the pinned block; a deep scroll animates only the final rows, and with animations off it settles without motion. A failed pin, an unpin, a remote pin change or a manual pinned-order move leaves the viewport where it was. A folder/search/account change or a new drag before the follow retires it, and the replacement view does not jump. The row context menu is anchored with 8dp spacing and highlights its row without selecting it. Hold until commands appear, then drag through adjacent rows before release: the original native range gesture remains active and no conversation opens. Stationary release transfers focus to the popup. Close and reopen the same row quickly: old command, dismissal and pointer callbacks must not affect the replacement opening. Repeat with TalkBack long-click, Back, and outside dismissal; scrolling the anchor out of composition or leaving Chats dismisses its menu. With two pins in a saved manual order, receive one and then several unpinned chat invites, then accept one and decline another; repeat in the Name and Unread folder sorts. The pins stay first in their manual order with a single divider below the last pin, the invites sit directly under that divider above ordinary chats, and accepting or declining never moves or unpins a pin. With system animations on, pin and unpin rows next to the divider under the last pin, in light, dark and AMOLED, and once in RTL with large text: the divider glides with the rows and never jumps a slot or draws across a row passing over it. Unpinning the last pin or pinning every visible chat removes the divider at once, leaving no line behind; with animations off it lands in its new slot together with the rows. With both pinned and unpinned chats present, pull the list down and let go before the filter threshold: the rows and divider settle back together; then pull past it to turn the unread filter on, and again to turn it off: each time the divider glides to its new slot with the rows. The menu follows the physical press as far as the window allows. TalkBack uses the row anchor.

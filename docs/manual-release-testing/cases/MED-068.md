1. [ ] **MED-068 — Edited photo drafts after a chat is deleted** — With a disposable account, attach a photo in chat A, change it in the photo editor, save it and leave it unsent.
    Do the same in chat B.
    Delete chat A from this device.
    Force-stop the app and relaunch it twice, then reopen chat B and open its draft photo in the editor.
    Repeat with a group you leave as its last member in place of the deleted chat.
    On a debug build, read the app log across both launches. → **Expected:** The app starts normally both times.
    Chat B's draft still holds its edited photo and the editor opens it as it did before the restart.
    Chat A does not return, and nothing from its draft appears in another chat.
    The log has no "photo editor reconciliation deferred" line on either launch.

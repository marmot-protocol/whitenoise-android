[ ] **NTF-038 — Notification tap opens the conversation without an intermediate surface** — Sign in two accounts, one with a long chat history and a peer-heavy chat list, make account B inactive, receive a message for B and tap it. Repeat in four lifecycles: with the app already running, after force-stopping it, with B's runtime still starting, and with app lock enabled. Screen-record each run at full frame rate and note the device, API level and distribution. → **Expected:** The conversation appears directly. At no point between the tap and the readable conversation does a loading screen, blank surface or the chat list draw, and the target account's chat list loads only after the conversation is readable. Warm taps take about one second or less, cold-process and not-ready taps stay within their budgets, and anything over two seconds on a warm tap is reported with the recording. With app lock on, no conversation content draws before unlock, the staged tap routes immediately afterwards, and human unlock time is not counted as route time. Budgets are device measurements, not assumptions, and an unmeasured lifecycle stays unmeasured.

1. [ ] **CHL-041 — Chat-list rows accept taps right after returning to the app** — With the chat list open at the top, send the app to the background. From another device, send messages to two chats lower in the list so one becomes the newest chat and another moves up. Return to the app and tap the newly promoted top row as soon as the list appears. Repeat with app lock enabled. → **Expected:** The list appears with the promoted chats already in their new positions, with no slide animation. The first tap opens the tapped chat without a delay or an ignored tap. Messages that arrive after the list is back on screen still move rows with the usual animation. With app lock enabled, the lock screen appears first and no chat-list content shows until it is cleared.

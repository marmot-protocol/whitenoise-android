[ ] **GRP-045 — A group change that was not made says so once** — This needs a disposable account that is an admin of a test group, a second admin on another device, and a second profile on the phone. (a) Rename the group, then switch on airplane mode straight away and keep White Noise open until it reports the change; MarmotKit gives up a change no relay received, which can take several minutes. (b) Once that notice has shown, force stop White Noise and reopen it. (c) With both admins online, change the group's name or members on both devices at the same moment, several times. (d) Go offline, start a group change so it waits to send, have the other admin remove your admin role, then come back online, and have them make you an admin again. (e) Start (a), switch to the second profile before White Noise reports the change and wait as long again, then switch back to the first profile and restart White Noise. (f) Repeat (a), switch to the second profile while its notice is still on screen, then switch back and restart White Noise. (g) Turn on app lock, repeat (a) and leave White Noise in the background until the lock applies, then return and unlock. Repeat with TalkBack, in RTL and at the largest font size. → **Expected:** Each change that was not made shows one notice titled with the chat's name that says what did not happen and why, for example Group details not updated with It didn't reach any of the group's relays, or You're no longer an admin of this group for (d). When the chat is not in the loaded chat list the notice names the change instead of the chat. The notice from (a) does not come back after the reopen in (b). In (e) nothing shows while the second profile is active; the change shows once when White Noise restarts with the first profile active, and not again after a further restart. In (f) the notice leaves the screen as the second profile opens and shows once after the restart. The notice from (g) shows after unlocking, never behind the lock screen. For (c), a change White Noise sent again shows nothing, and one another admin's change already made reads Another change to this group already did what yours would have. TalkBack reads the title and detail, and the notice is not clipped in RTL or at the largest font size.

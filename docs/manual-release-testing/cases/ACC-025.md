1. [ ] **ACC-025 — Offer to link after sign-in** — This needs the build on two devices and a disposable local-key account already signed in on the first. On the second device sign in to the same account and finish setup. When the chat list opens, choose Link this device, compare the emoji on both devices and finish the link. Sign out, sign in again and each time do one of: choose Not now; close the offer with Back; choose Not now and then Link this device on the chat list's reminder; choose Not now and then Dismiss on the reminder; choose Link this device, press Back on the step and reopen it from the reminder; choose Not now and link from Settings, Account, Linked devices instead; lock the app while the offer is showing; open search on the chat list while the reminder is showing; restart the app while the reminder is showing. Repeat with a newly created account, with an account that signs through an external signer app, and with a second profile active. Repeat in dark, AMOLED, RTL and at the largest font size, and with TalkBack. → **Expected:** During setup, the Chats on this device step says this device can be linked after sign-in and does not say its chats stay separate. After a sign-in that found the account on another installation, the chat list opens with one question, Link this device?, offering Link this device and Not now. Link this device opens the same steps as Settings, drawn behind the status and navigation bars with no grey bands, and asks the other device once, however often the step is closed and reopened. Not now and Back request nothing and leave a reminder at the top of the chat list with Link this device and Dismiss; the reminder survives a restart, hides during search and belongs only to the profile that signed in. Dismiss removes the reminder, and linking stays available under Linked devices. Once the device is linked, by either route, neither the offer nor the reminder shows again. Closing the linking steps after accepting the offer returns to the reminder, including after a restart. The offer waits while the app is locked. A newly created account and an external-signer account get no offer and no reminder. Nothing is clipped at the largest font size or in RTL, and both reminder actions stay reachable. If local removal completes but relay cleanup is incomplete, the cleanup report stays available after signing in again.

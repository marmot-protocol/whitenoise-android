[ ] **NTF-042 — Conversation picture in message notifications** — With previews on, have a disposable contact who has a public profile picture send you a direct message, then give that contact a private picture in their profile and have them send again; have a member send into a group that has a group picture, and into a group and a direct chat whose picture is missing or fails to load. For each, receive a short first message, a first message of at least 160 code points, a short message after the long one, and a long message after earlier messages. Inspect the heads-up banner, the collapsed and expanded shade card and the lock screen, on Android 12 or newer and on Android 11 if available. Repeat after clearing the private picture, after switching accounts with cards from both accounts visible, after dismissing a card while its picture loads, and with previews off and with app lock on. → **Expected:** A direct-message card's main picture is the contact's private picture when set, otherwise their public profile picture; a group card shows the group picture while each message keeps its sender's own face. The small app badge stays in its own small position and is never the main picture. A missing or failed picture shows the same stable lettered circle as the conversation's shortcut, never the White Noise logo or a member's face standing in for a group. The long-message text card keeps the same conversation picture, and so do the following short message and the long message behind earlier messages. Clearing the private picture falls back without showing the old private picture; cards from one account never show the other account's private pictures; a dismissed card is not brought back by a late picture. With previews off or app lock on, every card and the lock-screen version show only generic app text and no conversation, contact or group picture. Record the device, Android version and screenshots of each state; the very first card of a conversation that has never had a shortcut, or whose shortcut was hidden by turning previews off, may still show Android's app header until the next card, and that is recorded rather than failed.

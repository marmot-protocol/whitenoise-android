1. [ ] **CON-051 — Composer draft jump buttons** — With the default composer toolbar, type or paste a draft long enough to scroll inside the editor and leave the caret at the end. Tap Scroll to top, then Scroll to bottom, and drag the draft to a middle position. Shorten the draft to one line. Open Customize composer, move both jump buttons to the left side and reorder them, then remove one and finally both. Repeat with a long draft in RTL, at 200% text, with TalkBack, and hold each jump button. → **Expected:** Both jumps sit in the same row as the other composer buttons, never on a separate row. At the end only Scroll to top shows; at the start only Scroll to bottom shows; in the middle both show. Each tap moves the draft to that edge without changing text, caret or selection. A short draft shows neither button and the editor keeps their width. Customize composer lists both jumps, and the composer follows the chosen side, order and removal; removed jumps never appear. Holding a jump explains it, TalkBack reads its name, and RTL and 200% text keep the row aligned.

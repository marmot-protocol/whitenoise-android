1. [ ] **DIC-020 — Record dictations in different chats while transcription finishes** — With a slow caller-audio recognition result, dictate in one disposable chat and choose Send. While it processes, open a second chat, dictate a different phrase and choose Paste. If the first result is still pending, open a third chat, use hold-to-dictate and release over Send. → **Expected:** Each recording can start while the earlier one is processing. Results arrive in recording order and only in the chat where they were recorded. The second phrase appears in its draft and the other phrases are sent once.

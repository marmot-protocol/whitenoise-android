[ ] **MED-061 — Voice recording continues outside the chat** — Lock a voice recording, then open another chat and press its microphone, go back to the chat list, switch to another account and back, and return to the first chat. Stop the take, leave the chat and look at its chat-list row, then reopen it. Force-stop the app and reopen that chat. Lock a new take and press Home. Finally sign out of an account that has an unsent voice take. → **Expected:** The take keeps recording while you are in other chats and on the chat list; the other chat's microphone says "A voice message is recording in another chat." and records nothing. The chat-list row shows a red waveform labelled "Recording voice message" while recording and an accent waveform labelled "Unsent voice message" while the take waits; other accounts never show it. Returning shows the running strip with its timer, and a finished take waits in review, also after the force-stop. Pressing Home stops capture and keeps what was recorded as that chat's waiting take. Signing out deletes that account's unsent takes.

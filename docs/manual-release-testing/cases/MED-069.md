[ ] **MED-069 — Shared Media photo share recovers from leaving the app and an unloadable photo** — In a chat whose Photos and videos gallery has three photos from different senders, select all three in Shared Media and tap Share, then press Home before the share sheet appears and stay away until the photos have had time to download, then return to White Noise.
    Tap Share again and pick a receiving app.
    Then open two of the photos so they are on the device, leave the third undownloaded, select all three, turn on airplane mode and tap Share.
    Tap Share once more.
    → **Expected:** A share prepared while White Noise is in the background opens no share sheet later and shows no error; on return all three photos are still selected and the next Share sends all three.
    Returning before the share is prepared opens its share sheet as usual.
    The photo that cannot be loaded stops the share with "Couldn’t share photos" naming its sender, it is deselected while the other two stay selected, and the next Share sends those two.

1. [ ] **MED-048 — In-app audio handoff pauses and keeps its place** — Play a voice message, then start read-aloud on a text message. Start read-aloud again, then hold the mic to record. Start a locked recording, then start read-aloud from another message and tap Resume on the recording strip. Finally, start a recording and take a phone call. → **Expected:** Each interrupted sound pauses at once and never restarts by itself. The voice message keeps its position and plays on from there when tapped; read-aloud keeps its sentence. The interrupted recording shows Resume with a steady dot and its timer frozen; tapping Resume stops read-aloud and the timer continues from where it stopped, and the sent take contains both parts with no gap. A phone call during recording ends the take and opens its review.

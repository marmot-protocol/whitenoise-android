[ ] **NTF-058 — Quick access: chat-list button and Quick Settings tile** — In Settings → Notifications → Notification profiles look at Quick access, turn on Show in chat list and return to the chats; turn it off again. On Android 13 or later tap Add to Quick Settings and accept, then reopen the list. On an older version, add the Notification profile tile from the Quick Settings edit screen. Open Quick Settings with no profile on, tap the tile and pick a profile, then tap it and pick Off. Lock the phone with a PIN and tap the tile from the lock screen; turn on App Lock and repeat. Long-press the tile. Sign out of every account and open Quick Settings. → **Expected:** The chat-list profile button appears only while Show in chat list is on and a profile exists. Add to Quick Settings shows Android's prompt and the row disappears once the tile is added; before Android 13 only the hint shows. The tile reads Off when no profile is on and shows the active profile's emoji and name, or just On while App Lock is on, matching the banner. Picking a profile turns it on until turned off and Off turns profiles off, both at once; from the lock screen the phone asks to unlock first. With App Lock enabled the tile opens White Noise and the chooser appears only after unlock. Long-press opens White Noise. With nobody signed in the tile is unavailable.

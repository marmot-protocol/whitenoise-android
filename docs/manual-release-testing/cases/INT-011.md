1. [ ] **INT-011 — Avatar recovery** — With retained profile and group metadata, turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, force-stop the app without clearing its data, and reopen. Keep the missing profile/group avatars visible, then restore networking. Repeat while the app is backgrounded, return to it, and switch accounts while an avatar is loading. Then, with a profile picture still showing initials on an open profile, wait over a minute without changing connectivity and open a chat or the avatar viewer that loads the same picture. → **Expected:** Missing visible avatars retry when validated internet returns, without restarting the app, navigating away, or waiting for the previous failure cooldown. A backgrounded screen waits until visible before retrying. Already-loaded pictures do not flash back to initials; a late old-account image never replaces the selected identity. Once that other screen shows the picture, the profile still on screen shows it too without reopening or another download. A genuine remote image failure keeps the fallback without a polling loop. This check does not promise offline image persistence after process death.

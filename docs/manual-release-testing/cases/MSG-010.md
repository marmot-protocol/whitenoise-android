1. [ ] **MSG-010 — Forward one/many** — Forward a supported message to one chat and then multiple chats using search and direct selection. Next select an outside chat, use a folder chip with at least two eligible destinations, deselect one member directly, and tap the chip through mixed, On, and Off states before confirming. → **Expected:** Origin and ineligible chats are excluded. Off or mixed selects every eligible folder member; On removes only that folder's members and preserves the outside choice. The chip's count and checkbox state match the destination rows, progress names each target, every target receives one freshly sent payload, and no source encryption or author attribution leaks. Forward a text attachment from a source the device already holds (a received file opened earlier, and your own send) and from one it does not: the held source makes no new source download and the attachment still arrives once, a source whose retained copy is missing or unreadable still forwards by downloading it, a held source larger than the 32 MiB forward limit fails as too large without being downloaded again, and with local performance diagnostics enabled the forward's phases are recorded without any name, identifier or content. Send a text while offline or on a slow connection and, while it still shows Sending, forward it from its message menu and from a selection that also includes an older sent message; repeat with a selection that includes a photo that is still uploading. → **Expected:** A pending text forwards at once from its accepted text, without waiting for its own delivery, and each destination receives it once as a new message with no reply, sender attribution or temporary identity. The open picker keeps that text if the source then sends, fails or is cancelled, and none of those later changes rewrites, duplicates or cancels the accepted forward. A selection containing a pending send offers only Copy and Forward, and a still-uploading photo keeps Forward unavailable for the whole selection.

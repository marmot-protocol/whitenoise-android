[ ] **MED-030 — Large file and video sends** — On disposable accounts in a test direct chat and a test group, attach through Attach → Files a 100 MiB document and one just under 512 MiB, pick a 200 MiB video from the gallery together with two photos, and send each. Also try a file just over 512 MiB, and a reply that carries a 40 MiB document. Leave the chat while one upload runs; turn networking off during another, reconnect and tap Retry; delete a third while it is still uploading, and delete a fourth while it waits behind another large upload in the same chat. Change the theme or font size while a failed large send waits for Retry, then retry it. Repeat with less free storage than three times the combined size of an album's large videos, once with free storage only just enough for one large video followed by a short video or a draft photo, and once with a draft photo added after two large videos. Watch the app's memory in developer options during a large send, and watch the card of a single large document, the play ring of a single large video and an album with a large video while each sends, with TalkBack on for one of them. Try Share on a pending large message, and on the sender open the sent document and video with automatic downloads off. → **Expected:** Files and videos up to 512 MiB send without the "File too large to attach" notice and arrive once on the peer, who can download, open and save them; the album keeps its order and caption. The file over 512 MiB and the large reply attachment are refused with "File too large to attach". Leaving the chat does not stop the upload. A failed upload offers Retry, which resends the same file without picking it again, including after the theme or font change. Deleting a message during its upload, or while it waits behind another upload, removes it without a failure notice and nothing reaches the peer. Low storage, judged for the whole send including small items after a large video, reports that private storage is unavailable or full instead of failing during upload, and only the items that no longer fit are left out; a draft photo that no longer fits beside large videos is left out with "Selection too large to send" while the videos still send. Memory stays roughly flat during a large send instead of growing with the file size. A single large document's ring fills steadily while its size line reads Preparing, then Encrypting, then the bytes sent out of the file's size, then Sending, and TalkBack reads each step with its bytes. A single large video's play ring fills the same way. An album keeps its spinning ring without bytes. A failed large send drops the bytes and offers Retry, whose count starts again from Preparing. A pending large video shows its blurred preview, or a plain tile when none could be made, instead of a poster frame, and Share becomes available once the message is sent. When the device had room to keep a copy, the sender's own large document and video open without downloading again; otherwise they download like a received file. If the group's media server refuses a large upload, the send fails with Retry rather than crashing. While a large file uploads, other sends in the same chat wait for it to finish.

[ ] **MED-030 — Large file and video sends** — On disposable accounts in a test direct chat and a test group, attach through Attach → Files a 100 MiB document and one just under 512 MiB, pick a 200 MiB video from the gallery together with two photos, and send each. Also try a file just over 512 MiB, and a reply that carries a 40 MiB document. Leave the chat while one upload runs; turn networking off during another, reconnect and tap Retry; delete a third while it is still uploading, and delete a fourth while it waits behind another large upload in the same chat. Change the theme or font size while a failed large send waits for Retry, then retry it. Repeat one send with less free storage than three times the file size. Watch the app's memory in developer options during a large send, try Share on a pending large message, and on the sender open the sent document and video with automatic downloads off. → **Expected:** Files and videos up to 512 MiB send without the "File too large to attach" notice and arrive once on the peer, who can download, open and save them; the album keeps its order and caption. The file over 512 MiB and the large reply attachment are refused with "File too large to attach". Leaving the chat does not stop the upload. Deleting a message during its upload, or while it waits behind another upload, removes it without a failure notice and nothing reaches the peer. Low storage reports that private storage is unavailable or full. Memory stays roughly flat during a large send instead of growing with the file size. A pending large video shows its name and dimensions instead of a poster frame, and Share becomes available once the message is sent. When the device had room to keep a copy, the sender's own large document and video open without downloading again; otherwise they download like a received file. If the group's media server refuses a large upload, the send fails with Retry rather than crashing. While a large file uploads, other sends in the same chat wait for it to finish.

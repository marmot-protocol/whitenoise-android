[ ] **SEC-018 — Audit delivery status and local retention** — In a disposable fixture with multiple profiles and diagnostic sharing enabled, arrange a receiver refusal for one profile (401, 403 or 404). Open Diagnostics & Improvements, cold-restart the fixture, refresh, and then tap Resume delivery after making the receiver available. Also check a retryable failure, an unconfigured receiver and sharing turned off. Open Keep audit logs for, cancel a shorter period, then confirm 7 days, reopen the screen and restart. Try exporting a selection after one of its files has expired. → **Expected:** A persisted pause appears even if another profile is healthy. Opening or refreshing the status reads local data only. Resume requests one MDK delivery pass and rereads the actual result, showing Paused again if the receiver still refuses it. Sharing off offers no send action. The retention row shows MDK’s 30-day default or its stored value. Shortening warns that older files may be removed even if they have not uploaded. Cancel makes no write. MDK persists the confirmed age, preserves the per-profile size budget and sweeps eligible files. An expired selection shows a retry without an incomplete export. Verify both controls are reachable through Settings search and legible in RTL and at large text sizes.

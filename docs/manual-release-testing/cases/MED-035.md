[ ] **MED-035 — Received media when storage is full** — On a disposable receiver, fill the device until less free space remains than a received attachment needs plus MarmotKit's publication reserve. Receive a document, a photo, an album of photos, a video and a voice message, with automatic downloads on, and also tap Download on each while space is still short. Then free space without touching the chat, and separately free space and tap Retry. → **Expected:** Each attachment shows a storage problem, never a generic failure or an endless spinner: the file card reads "Not enough storage" in the error color with a Retry control, a large photo shows a Retry control captioned "Not enough storage. Free up space, then tap to retry.", and grid tiles, video and voice show Retry whose spoken description says the same. Freeing space resumes each download on its own within about a minute, from its checkpoint where one exists, and Retry resumes it at once. While a visible attachment resumes, it shows its progress and Cancel instead of the storage message, and if it then fails for another reason it shows the ordinary failure, not "Not enough storage". With automatic downloads off, tap Download on a photo, a video and a voice message while space is short, leave the chat, return while it is still short, and then free space: each still shows the storage message on return, never a plain Download, and loads by itself once space frees, with no second tap. Then let an automatic photo download park for storage, leave the chat, turn photo auto-download off, reopen the chat and free space: the photo offers Download and downloads nothing until it is tapped. No attachment is reported as failed for good, and no other attachment or message is affected. Verify light/dark/AMOLED, RTL and 200% text. On a disposable sender using the nightly, exhaust the space needed for native attachment staging and send a video or document. → **Expected:** The send reports “Not enough storage to send. Free up space and try again.”, keeps its pending attachment bytes, and does not publish the refused upload. Copy the send failure report: it identifies `error=STORAGE_FULL` and `marmot=StorageFull`, without native exception text, file paths or wrapper details. Free space and retry the same pending item: it sends once without selecting the source again. Qualify forwarding separately because native staging also applies to forwarded uploads. Repeat with an album that mixes a large video picked from Files and an edited photo, filling the disk so the app's own snapshot of the photo cannot be written when the upload starts. → **Expected:** The same storage notice appears instead of a generic send failure, its report reads `error=STORAGE_FULL`, the pending album keeps its items, and Retry sends once after space is freed. On a disposable sender with about 2.2 times a 400 MB video free plus a small margin, pick that video from Files and send it. → **Expected:** It is admitted and sends; a large file-backed send now needs about twice its size free rather than three times.

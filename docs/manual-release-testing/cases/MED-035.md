[ ] **MED-035 — Received media when storage is full** — On a disposable receiver, fill the device until less free space remains than a received attachment needs plus MarmotKit's publication reserve. Receive a document, a photo, an album of photos, a video and a voice message, with automatic downloads on, and also tap Download on each while space is still short. Then free space without touching the chat, and separately free space and tap Retry. → **Expected:** Each attachment shows a storage problem, never a generic failure or an endless spinner: the file card reads "Not enough storage" in the error color with a Retry control, a large photo shows a Retry control captioned "Not enough storage. Free up space, then tap to retry.", and grid tiles, video and voice show Retry whose spoken description says the same. Freeing space resumes each download on its own within about a minute, from its checkpoint where one exists, and Retry resumes it at once. While a visible attachment resumes, it shows its progress and Cancel instead of the storage message, and if it then fails for another reason it shows the ordinary failure, not "Not enough storage". No attachment is reported as failed for good, and no other attachment or message is affected. Verify light/dark/AMOLED, RTL and 200% text.

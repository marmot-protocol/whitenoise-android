1. [ ] **ONB-029 — Remote signer account status and recovery** — Keep a local-key account, an Amber account and two remote-signer accounts on one device. Open Settings → Account keys for a remote-signer account, then send a message and create a group with the signer online. Take the signer offline, try to send, and open the account switcher. Bring it back and use Retry connection. Force-stop and reopen the app with the signer online, then with it offline. Use Disconnect signer and confirm, then Pair again with the same signer and once with a signer for another identity. Switch between all four accounts throughout. → **Expected:** Account keys shows Signed in with a remote signer and Connected instead of a private-key row; Amber accounts still show their Amber notice. Permitted sends and group creation succeed. An offline signer shows Signer offline with Retry connection and Pair again, the switcher marks only that account with Remote signer needs attention, and no other account prompts, reconnects or shows that status. A restart restores the account without pairing again; offline restoration still opens the app and the account. Disconnect keeps the account and its history and pauses signing until Pair again succeeds; pairing with a different identity is refused and leaves the account unchanged. The Amber account keeps using Amber after every restart.

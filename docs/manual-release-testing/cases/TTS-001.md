1. [ ] **TTS-001 — Text to speech** — Choose Speak on a text message, pause/resume, change rate, move one sentence or one message backward/forward, and stop. Start read-aloud with the two-finger downward swipe over incoming and outgoing plain, Markdown, reply, long/collapsed and mixed-content messages; repeat at 200% text and in a short window. Enable Speak over playing media, start read-aloud with other media stopped, then repeat while music or a podcast is playing. → **Expected:** Preparing, speaking, pausing, resuming, seeking and stopping never change the message row or bubble's natural width or height; only the read-aloud highlight, progress announcement and transport state change. The compact top transport has a single control row plus message progress and estimated seconds remaining, with no message-title/detail rows. Tapping blank transport space returns to the active message. The current sentence is fully visible; when its bounds leave the viewport, follow-along places it at the top. A direct drag suspends movement for the current sentence, while the next spoken sentence resumes following. The correct content is highlighted/spoken, the group header or profile/account quick picker stays at the top with playback directly underneath, including starting voice or read-aloud, Back to the chat list, reopening the source conversation and stopping playback; neither header shifts vertically when playback appears or disappears. The transport remains accessible across chat/list navigation, and Stop clears playback. With no other media, read-aloud starts normally with ordinary audio focus. With other media playing, speech mixes at the selected level without controlling that playback. At 200% RTL and short window height, horizontally scroll the compact control row to every action; the first chat row remains visible and operable below it.

1. [ ] **TTS-001 — Text to speech** — Choose Speak on a text message, pause/resume, change rate, move one sentence or one message backward/forward, and stop. Start read-aloud with the two-finger downward swipe over incoming and outgoing plain, Markdown, reply, long/collapsed and mixed-content messages; repeat at 200% text and in a short window. Enable Speak over playing media, start read-aloud with other media stopped, then repeat while music or a podcast is playing. → **Expected:** Ordinary message rows keep their natural width and height across Preparing, speaking, pausing, resuming, seeking and stopping. A collapsed long message expands inline when its verified speech starts, so every sentence can be highlighted and revealed; no full-screen view opens automatically. Long-press a noninitial sentence in the collapsed excerpt and choose Speak: the selected sentence must be read and revealed directly beneath the player after expansion, without pressing Resume. Read beyond the former collapse cutoff, pause and seek there, advance to another message, stop, and scroll the row away and back. Previously revealed rows remain expanded without a collapse jump. A replacement speech session or edited/deleted source cannot reuse stale expansion or highlighting. The compact top transport has a single control row plus message progress and estimated seconds remaining, with no message-title/detail rows. Tapping blank transport space returns to the active message. The current sentence is fully visible; when its bounds leave the viewport, follow-along places it at the top. A direct drag preserves the chosen viewport across sentences, messages, seeks, Preparing and pause/resume until the target-icon Resume follow control is pressed. Resume reveals the current sentence directly beneath the player/header without starting paused audio. A fully visible sentence stays still; repeat with tall mixed-content rows and asymmetric viewport padding. The correct content is highlighted/spoken, the group header or profile/account quick picker stays at the top with playback directly underneath, including starting voice or read-aloud, Back to the chat list, reopening the source conversation and stopping playback; neither header shifts vertically when playback appears or disappears. The transport remains accessible across chat/list navigation, and Stop clears playback. With no other media, read-aloud starts normally with ordinary audio focus. With other media playing, speech mixes at the selected level without controlling that playback. At 200% RTL and short window height, horizontally scroll the compact control row to every action; the first chat row remains visible and operable below it. While reading, double-tap a later sentence in a queued non-current message and an unqueued visible message, then repeat with rapid competing taps, unmappable text and paused playback. The exact sentence becomes current in the same session; a stale or failed target preserves current playback. A link double-tap seeks its spoken neighbor; a single tap still opens it. Scroll the current sentence partly and entirely out of view, advance several sentences without engine word callbacks, rotate and show/hide the player. Resume follow remains available whenever the active sentence cannot be confirmed fully visible, including after a failed reveal; pressing it reveals the current sentence without replaying paused audio. Text selection preserves manual viewport ownership until Resume is pressed. After usable native timing in one utterance, a silent next utterance still shows estimated words; delayed native callbacks from the old utterance never move the new cursor. Mentions retain the name used by the active speech projection while playing; a new session uses the latest profile name. Edited/deleted messages reject old highlighting and geometry. Background the app, open another app, lock/unlock, rotate, and return during an auto-read queue. Deliver several incoming messages together and repeat the projection while speaking and paused. All eligible arrivals must join the same chronological queue exactly once, including while the source screen is absent; paused arrivals wait for Resume within the documented retention budget. A lost native feed, unrecoverable timeline gap or exceeded retention budget stops and discards speech; Resume cannot replay disconnected text, and a fresh start reloads authoritative content. Interrupt synthesis and Resume: retry the earliest unfinished sentence without skipping to a later prebuffered utterance. Permanent focus loss retains the paused queue. Stop, notification dismissal, recents removal, account replacement and app-lock policy revoke speech and late callbacks. OS process death starts idle and persists no decrypted speech queue.

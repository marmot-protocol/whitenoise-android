1. [ ] **SET-047 — Manage storage overview and Keep media** — Use an account with several chats holding attachments. Open Settings → Data & storage and tap Manage storage at the top, then read the usage panel with and without TalkBack. Tap the Attachments segment of the bar, then tap it again; tap the thinnest segment; then tap the Messages and chat data legend row. Change Keep media from a longer window to a shorter one and confirm, then to a longer window. With Manage storage open, switch to another account. Search Settings for "storage", "largest attachments", "keep media" and "clear temporary files", and for the name of one of your chats. Repeat the overview and the Keep media confirmation in dark theme, RTL and large text. → **Expected:** The Data & storage row reads "Manage storage" with "See what's stored on this device and free up space" and opens the screen; Back returns to Data & storage. The panel shows this profile's total, which leaves out the temporary files all profiles share, a bar, and a legend naming Attachments (with its count), Messages and chat data, Profile and group pictures and Temporary files ("Shared by all profiles on this device") with sizes, plus Unused database space only when there is some. It pulses under "Calculating…" until figures arrive. TalkBack reads the header as "Storage used: …" and each legend row as its own sentence with its size and share. Tapping a segment or legend row highlights that category: the other segments and dots fade, its row takes a tonal fill, and the header shows its size with "Attachments · N% of <total>" (temporary files say they are shared by all profiles and not in this total); TalkBack announces the change and reports the row as selected. Tapping the same category again restores the total, and a thin segment can be picked by tapping on or right beside it. A shorter Keep media window asks first, naming the window, and removes older attachments; a longer one applies at once with "Keep media setting saved". After the account switch no figure, chat or selection from the first account remains. The four searches find Manage storage, Largest attachments, Keep media and Clear pictures and temporary files; chat names and attachments never appear in Settings search. Dark theme, RTL and large text keep every label, size and button readable, unclipped and correctly ordered.

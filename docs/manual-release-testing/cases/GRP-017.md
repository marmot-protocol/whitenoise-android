1. [ ] **GRP-017 — Disappearing messages** — Choose Off, a preset, and custom duration in a disposable group; send messages and wait/advance to expiry. After a process restart, let the hourly retention worker run once with a signed-in account and once with no signed-in account on a backed-up disposable installation; compare native-start attempts and network activity. → **Expected:** Current policy is visible, only messages under the active policy expire, and expiration removes content consistently without affecting newer exemptions. The signed-in worker still prunes expired messages after process death; the empty-account worker finishes without starting notification or relay runtime, and an uncertain account read retries. After a message expires, searching for its text from inside the conversation and from the chat list returns nothing, and its attachments no longer appear in the shared-media browser, even before the next hourly prune. A conversation with no visible messages and a policy in force explains the policy and its duration rather than showing the plain empty state, updates when the policy changes, and never claims messages were lost. The disappearing-messages explainer states that the selected duration applies to new messages whether they have been read or not and that expiry permanently removes them for everyone; it never says messages are deleted after being seen or read. Leave a second chat closed with an expiring message whose notification is still showing, and let the hourly worker run after the deadline: the notification is dismissed, and on opening the chat the message and its attachment are gone.

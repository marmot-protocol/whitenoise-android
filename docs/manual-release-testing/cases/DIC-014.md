1. [ ] **DIC-014 — Continuous dictation with a selected speech service** — Set the integrated offline speech service version 61 or later as Android's selected recognition service. With Pause other audio enabled and cooperative music playing, start dictation, speak for over 30 seconds, and keep speaking while earlier phrases decode. Repeat with a pause and then a distinctive final phrase, choosing Paste and Send separately; cancel another attempt. Repeat with an unknown selected provider and on Android below 13. → **Expected:** On Android 13 or later the supported service receives caller-captured audio without an empty-audio probe. Music stays paused and speech recorded during decoding appears in order after completion. Paste or Send closes the recorder, resumes music promptly, and delivers the complete text once. Cancel resumes music once, leaves the draft unchanged, and rejects late results. Other selected providers and older Android versions keep their native microphone path without a new capability probe; their existing processing and recovery bounds remain effective.

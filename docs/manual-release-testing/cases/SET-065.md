1. [ ] **SET-065 — This device is unlinked, and signing out with linked devices** — This needs a disposable account linked on devices A and B, with a second profile signed in on B. On A remove device B while B has the linked account active, and again while B has the other profile active. With no other profile on B, choose Remove from this device on the prompt. Repeat, and this time force-stop B instead, reopen it and choose Remove for the account on the sign-in screen, first cancelling the confirmation once. Then sign in to the account again and link B anew. Separately, on a linked device use Sign out and wipe while the other device is online, and again while the other device is offline. → **Expected:** B shows This device was unlinked once. If another profile is signed in, B moves to it with a notice naming the unlinked account, and that profile and its chats are untouched. With no other profile, a prompt that cannot be dismissed covers the chats, explains that removing deletes the account's data from this device only, and offers Remove from this device; choosing it shows the wipe progress and lands on sign-in with the account gone. After a restart the sign-in screen offers Remove for the account instead of Continue as, behind the same explanation, and Cancel changes nothing. A device removed from one chat only is not told it was unlinked. Signing in again afterwards is an ordinary new sign-in that offers to link this device. Signing out with the other device online reports the chats left for the other devices to finish separately from the chats this device left. With the other device offline, the report says the other devices couldn't be asked and may still list this device, and it is shown as an issue rather than a clean sign-out.

[ ] **CON-046 — Dragged images into the composer** — On a device or emulator with split-screen or freeform windows, open a chat beside a gallery or file app. Drag one image, then several images at once (more than the attachment limit), onto the composer. Repeat with a slow or cloud-backed source (for example a not-yet-downloaded cloud photo), and while that copy is still running, scroll the conversation and type. Drop again before the first copy finishes. Start another slow drop and switch chat, switch account, or leave the conversation before it finishes. → **Expected:** The composer accepts the drop at once and the UI stays responsive while images are read; Send waits until the copy finishes. Dropped images appear as staged attachments for the chat where they were dropped, and concurrent drops never exceed the combined attachment limit (the overflow notice appears instead). Non-image items in the drop are ignored. A drop that is interrupted by a chat, account or screen change never appears in another chat or account and leaves no staged attachment behind. An unreadable source shows the existing could-not-read notice.

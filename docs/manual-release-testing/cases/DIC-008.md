1. [ ] **DIC-008 — Dictation Send with staged attachments** — In a disposable chat, share an image or document from another app so it is staged in the composer. Start dictation, say a short phrase and choose Send while dictation is active. Repeat by finishing with Paste, editing the text and pressing the ordinary Send. Repeat with two staged attachments, with a reply target, and while an attachment is still preparing. Repeat once more with a hardware or on-screen keyboard: choose dictation Send and, before the message appears, press the keyboard's Send or Enter key in the composer. → **Expected:** The staged attachments and the final text are sent together as one message, exactly as an ordinary Send would, and the composer clears only what was sent. While attachments are still preparing, or if the send is refused, nothing is published: the dictated text returns to the draft with the attachments still staged, ready for Send. Dictation never publishes a text-only message while attachments are staged, and a reply target is kept. Pressing the keyboard Send or Enter key while a dictation Send is still in progress does nothing extra: the attachments are sent once, in one message, and are never duplicated.

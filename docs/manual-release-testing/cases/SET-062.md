1. [ ] **SET-062 — Messages from your other devices** — This needs two linked devices on one disposable account, a peer on a current build, and a new chat with that peer created after linking. From device A send text, a photo and a reply; from the peer send a message. On device B open the chat, hold one of A's messages, then edit it and delete another. Put device B in the background and send from A again, then send from the peer. Turn device B's network off, send from B, and turn it back on. → **Expected:** On device B, messages sent from device A appear on the same side and with the same styling as B's own messages, with a sent mark and never a clock, a failed mark or Retry. A's photo downloads on B and shows progress like a received photo. B can edit and delete A's messages, because they belong to the same account, and the change shows on A and for the peer. Device B posts no notification, sound or vibration for A's messages, in the foreground or background, while the peer's message still notifies. Only the message B itself sent while offline shows as pending and then sent.

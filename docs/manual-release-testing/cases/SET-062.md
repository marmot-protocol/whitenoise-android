[ ] **SET-062 — Move a linked device to another set of devices** — This needs one disposable account with three devices: A and B linked to each other, and C linked on its own or to a fourth device. On B open Settings, Account, Linked devices, scroll to Advanced, choose Move this device and complete the emoji comparison with C. Watch Linked devices on B during and after the move. Force-stop and reopen B while the move is still joining. Tap Dismiss reminder for device A. Repeat, cancelling the comparison, and repeat with C's app out of date. → **Expected:** An account that already has a linked device is offered Move this device, not Link this device, as the last row of the screen under Advanced. While B moves, Linked devices shows Moving this device with Waiting to be added by the other devices or Joining the other devices, and never says the device moved until the move is complete. After a restart the same state is shown again. No further move is offered while one is pending. Once complete it says This device moved to the other devices and lists C. A reminder says device A is still in the earlier set; Dismiss reminder hides it and neither links nor removes A, which A's own list confirms. A cancelled or failed move says the move didn't finish and that this device still trusts its earlier devices, and B's list still shows A. B's chats stay throughout.

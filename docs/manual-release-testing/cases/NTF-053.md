1. [ ] **NTF-053 — Notification profiles with several accounts** — With two signed-in accounts, edit a profile while account A is active and allow a group that both accounts belong to. Switch to account B and open the same profile. Sign out of account A. → **Expected:** The editor notes which account the chats are chosen for. Messages to that group for account A notify during the profile; the same group for account B does not. Account B shows no allowed chats. After signing out of A the profile remains, and A's allowed chats are gone. Deleting a profile asks for confirmation and removes it from the list.

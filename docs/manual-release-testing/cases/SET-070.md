1. [ ] **SET-070 — History backups: turn on with recovery words, confirm, turn off** — Open Settings → Account → History backups on a signed-in account. On a build whose MarmotKit has backups disabled, note the status and callout. On a build with backups available, tap Turn on backups, try a screenshot and open Recents, tap Copy words and paste them into a password manager, tap I saved them, enter both requested words with one incorrect, tap Turn on backups, check the mismatch error, then tap Cancel. Start again, confirm with the two requested words, and reopen the screen. Tap Get new recovery words and cancel, then Turn off backups and confirm. Repeat in dark theme, RTL and the largest font size. → **Expected:** A build without backups shows "Not available yet" with the explanation and offers no setup or restore. Twelve numbered words appear once; screenshots and the Recents thumbnail are blocked on every page of the screen. Copy words pastes all twelve in order, separated by spaces; the button then reads Copied, Android shows no clipboard preview of the words, and the clipboard is cleared about a minute later. A wrong word shows the mismatch error and lets you try again; the words are never shown again after I saved them, and Cancel returns to the status page with backups unchanged. Confirming the requested words turns backups on and the status says this device saves them, with the daily charging-on-Wi-Fi note. Leaving setup and returning shows new words, never the earlier ones. Turning off asks first, then shows Off. Text wraps without clipping in every theme, direction and font size.

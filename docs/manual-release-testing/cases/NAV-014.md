1. [ ] **NAV-014 — Chat details screens across lock, rotation and restart** — Open a group chat, open its details, then open Group relays. Lock the app with App Lock and unlock it. Switch the system theme between light and dark. Press Back once. Open Group relays again, put the app in the background and let Android stop the process (or use Developer options → Don't keep activities together with a process kill), then reopen White Noise. Finally open Settings → Appearance and press Back twice. → **Expected:** After unlocking and after the theme change, Group relays is still shown for the same chat, and Back returns to the chat's details, then the chat, then the chat list. After the process restart the chat's details are shown, not Group relays. From Appearance, Back returns to Settings, then to the chat list, without any screen of the previous step briefly reappearing.

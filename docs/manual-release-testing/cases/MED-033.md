[ ] **MED-033 — Unobstructed photo and video frames with reachable playback controls** — Prepare local edge-marked fixtures: a portrait video, a landscape video, a video whose file carries rotation metadata, a tall photo and a wide photo, each with a distinct thin colour line along all four edges. Send them to a test chat, then open each in the full-screen viewer from the conversation, from a mixed photo and video album (paging between photo and video pages), from a video reached by swiping from a photo, and from Media library. Check portrait and landscape, a display cutout where available, large text, light, dark and AMOLED, and RTL. On a video page, wait for playback, tap the picture, drag the time bar to roughly the middle and release, then pause, let it end and replay. Repeat once while a voice note or read-aloud plays so the shell playback bar sits above the viewer. Open More on a playing video and wait a few seconds before choosing Save. With TalkBack on, find the video and use its show/hide controls action. Compare with the composer's pre-send video preview separately. → **Expected:** At normal zoom every photo and video shows all four edge lines, letterboxed rather than cropped or stretched, and rotated video plays upright. Visible viewer bars never cover the picture: the picture sits between the top bar and the Share/Forward bar, and Media3's time bar, elapsed and total time and play/pause sit above Share/Forward, clear of the navigation bar, cutout and shell playback bar. One tap on a video hides its controls and the viewer bars together so the frame can grow; the next tap brings both back. While playing, both hide after a few seconds; paused, ended and newly opened videos show them until dismissed, and paging to a video shows them again. Dragging the time bar seeks to the released position without switching pages, hiding controls or toggling the bars; a drag on the picture still switches pages. Buffering shows a spinner, and a clip of unknown length cannot be scrubbed to a guessed time. Download progress shown before a video plays is distinct from the playback time bar. The More menu stays open until dismissed even if the controls time out. Photo single tap, pinch, pan and double-tap reset behave as before, and hidden bars stay hidden while paging between photos. Rotation, bar changes and window resizing keep the same page, playback position and play/pause state; Close and Back each leave the viewer once. On a short landscape window the controls may drop the time text but keep the time bar and play/pause usable. Device verification with real decoders is required; the automated companion uses a scripted Media3 player.

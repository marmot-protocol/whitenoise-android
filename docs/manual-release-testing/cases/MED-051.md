1. [ ] **MED-051 — Fixed-size video in the viewer** — Open a portrait video and a landscape video in the full-screen viewer, in portrait and in landscape. Note where the video's edges sit, then tap the picture while it plays, tap again, and let playback hide the controls by itself. Pause and tap the picture twice. Drag the seek bar to roughly the middle and release. Open More on a playing video and wait a few seconds. Swipe to a photo page and tap it twice. Repeat once with large text and once with TalkBack on, moving through the video controls. → **Expected:** The video never moves or changes size when the controls or viewer bars appear, hide or time out, playing or paused, in either orientation. Play/pause, the seek bar and elapsed and total time are compact, sit inside the screen's safe area above Share/Forward and fade with the viewer bars. Paused videos keep their controls until tapped. Dragging the seek bar seeks without switching pages. With More open the top bar stays shown. Photo pages also stay put when the bars toggle. TalkBack announces play/pause, the playback position and both times, and the controls do not time out while TalkBack is on.

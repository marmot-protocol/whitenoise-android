Repeat after granting permission in app settings, returning through a notification for another chat or with a profile covering the original chat; dismiss that profile, press Home and resume within 30 seconds; also wait over 30 seconds before returning. Cancel, a still-blocked return, Home, account/chat replacement or explicit dismissal prevents a later unrelated unmute from starting capture. Runtime permission uses Android’s permission prompt; permanent denial opens Android app settings directly. OEM/hardware-policy suppression may leave the inline blocked state; verify the actual system prompt on the target device. Paste adds spoken words once.

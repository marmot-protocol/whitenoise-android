1. [ ] **CON-043 — Voice note placement, Send and Plus text actions** — Open Settings → Appearance → Composer on a fresh configuration and after Reset. Choose each Voice note placement: On Send, Left, Right and Off. In a chat, check an empty draft, typed text and an attachment for each choice; also unpin Emoji, Paste and Dictation and open Plus. Upgrade over a saved version-2 layout with and without a pinned voice note. → **Expected:** Fresh and reset settings show On Send selected, with Plus and Emoji on the physical left. On Send turns the empty composer's Send position into the hold-to-record microphone and changes it to Send once there is text or an attachment. Left and Right pin the voice note on that physical side, including RTL; Send stays visible in its own position, grey and inactive while the draft is empty. Off shows no voice note and keeps an inactive Send visible. The editor preview matches each choice. Plus lists every unpinned Emoji, Paste and Dictation action after the hidden attachments, and Plus stays available while any of them is unpinned. Paste from Plus inserts clipboard text or offers the system paste menu when access is denied; Dictation and Emoji from Plus behave as their shortcuts. Upgraded version-2 layouts keep their earlier pinned or hidden voice note.

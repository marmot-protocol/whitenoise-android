1. [ ] **NTF-009 — Notification tap** — Prepare a group chat containing exactly Alice and Carol, keep Bob's account active, and tap a synthetic Alice notification for that group. Leave the revealed conversation mounted, background the app, receive another synthetic notification, and tap it to return to the same conversation. Repeat with a disposable cold fixture that has no cached Alice member list, including a tap while the previous account screen is still settling. Automated companion coverage injects a deterministic roster-read failure before returning members, then lets the same reader succeed and taps Retry; ordinary offline mode alone does not prove that branch. It also injects the engine's exact terminal eviction response during route startup. While the recovered route remains open, have Carol add Bob and remove him again. Also open a fully loaded empty conversation from the chat list without a notification tap, keep it foregrounded, and have its peer send the first message. → **Expected:** The app binds Alice and the exact group before exposing its transcript; Bob's member list or messages never flash. The repeated tap owns its fresh request without recreating the already-mounted conversation controller. While the transcript is hidden or a roster Retry is showing, fresh target messages remain notification-eligible; after the authoritative transcript is revealed, its visible route suppresses them. An unknown cold member list shows loading followed by a recoverable error, rather than guessed sender chrome, and Retry recovers on the same route. Terminal eviction stops loading, shows the existing removed-member notice and no editable composer. The two-member compact chrome changes to ordinary group sender gutters at three members and returns at two, while the chat remains a group and its message/scroll state and unrelated notifications remain intact; only the tapped target clears appropriately. An ordinary settled empty conversation suppresses its foreground peer notification, and the first incoming row still receives normal initial positioning. A notification-routed group reveals its transcript once its roster is known, and that read runs alongside the conversation's timeline and group-state opens rather than after them, so a tap into a group whose roster is cold is no slower than the slowest single read. Tap a notification for a conversation on the already-active account while that conversation is momentarily absent from the visible chat list — for example straight after a cold start, before the list has caught up: the tap opens the conversation once the exact per-group read finds it, and never reports that the conversation is no longer available on the strength of the list not showing it yet. A message tap lands on the beginning of the notified message with the unread divider kept above it, and the route draws no loading or chat-list surface between the tap and the conversation: see NTF-037 and NTF-038.

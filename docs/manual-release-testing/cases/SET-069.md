[ ] **SET-069 — The avatar and the pen carry Chats into Settings and back** — With one signed-in account, tap the chat-list avatar to open Settings, with the New message pen on screen, then press Back (top-bar arrow and system Back). Add a second account and repeat through the avatar's account selector and its Settings button. Repeat in light, dark and AMOLED themes, in RTL, at the largest font size, in the two-pane layout and after rotating while Settings is open. Reverse mid-open and mid-close, and set Developer options → Animator duration scale to Off. Record the transition with a screen recorder and step through it frame by frame. → **Expected:** Both moves follow one smooth curve together. The avatar itself (picture or initials, never a coloured blob) flies from the top corner into its place in the Settings profile card, growing on the way, with the profile slot taking the surrounding card colour until the avatar lands. At the same time the New message pen morphs into the bottom-most Settings card on screen (Sign out, or the lowest card visible if it is below the fold, clipped like that card) the way the pen grows into the composer: a thin outline over the card colour, the pen glyph fading out as the card content fades in. There is only ever one avatar and one pen: the top-bar avatar and the pen button disappear as they leave, and the Settings avatar appears only when the flying one lands. The Settings header and other cards scale and fade in as a stack from the top edge of the moving pen contour. The chat list fades out quickly in place, with no dim or scrim. AMOLED shows see-through light outlines; light and dark show faint pills in the card colour with hairline edges that hand over to the cards at rest. Back is the exact reverse, slightly faster: the stack scales and fades toward the pen, the avatar flies back to the top corner, the bottom card morphs back into the pen button and the chat list fades in. In RTL the avatar starts in the other corner and the pen on the other side. Without the pen (two-pane layout or the pen hidden), Settings fades in place while the measured avatar still flies. If only the profile card is visible, the pen lands there and the avatar flies into the masked profile slot. The account selector's Settings button flies the active avatar the same way; an entry such as the app update control grows an outline into the card nearest it instead, and the pen still morphs into the bottom card when it is available. If the activated control claims that card, the pen stays in place and fades with Chats. Tapping or pressing Back mid-flight turns both around from the drawn frame without a jump. With animations off, both directions switch at once. After rotation or a pane change, Back still lands on the avatar or settles without motion; it never animates toward a stale position. Chats with screenshots blocked stay protected: no captured Chats image appears during the transition (the avatar travels as its live drawing), and Back pressed during the open reverses it instead of acting on the chat list.

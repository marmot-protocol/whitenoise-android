1. [ ] **SET-074 — History backups: turn on with recovery words, confirm, turn off** — Open Settings → Data & storage → History backups on a signed-in account. On a build whose MarmotKit has backups disabled, note the status and callout. On a build with backups available, read the storage and recovery guidance before switching Backups on, then tap the eye icon to reveal the words. Try a screenshot and open Recents, tap the copy icon and paste them into a password manager, tap I saved them, enter both requested words with one incorrect, check the inline word feedback while typing and tap Done, then tap Cancel. Start again, confirm with the two requested words, and reopen the screen. Tap Get new recovery words and cancel, then switch Backups off and confirm. Repeat in dark theme, RTL and the largest font size. → **Expected:** A build without backups shows "Not available yet" with the explanation and offers no setup or restore. Before the switch, the page explains that encrypted history is stored on Blossom file servers and Nostr relays hold the encrypted reference. It limits restore to chats this device is in and explains how to get invited back. Twelve numbered words stay masked until revealed and appear once; screenshots and the Recents thumbnail are blocked on every page of the screen. Copy words pastes all twelve in order, separated by spaces; the button then reads Copied, Android shows no clipboard preview of the words, and the clipboard is cleared about a minute later. A correct prefix stays neutral, a complete correct word shows a checkmark, and a wrong entry shows an error icon in its field. Submitting a wrong word shows the mismatch error and lets you try again; the words are never shown again after I saved them, and Cancel returns to the status page with backups unchanged. Confirming the requested words turns backups on and the status says this device saves them, with the daily charging-on-Wi-Fi note. Leaving setup and returning shows new words, never the earlier ones. Turning off asks first, then shows Off. Text wraps without clipping in every theme, direction and font size.

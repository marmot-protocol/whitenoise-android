[ ] **MED-058 — Automatic voice-message transcription** — On an Android 13+ installation with Offline Speech to Text and a speech model ready, open Settings → Dictation and turn on Transcribe voice messages automatically; long-press it to read its explanation. Receive several voice messages in a group and a direct chat with automatic downloads on, then with downloads off (download one by hand). Scroll quickly through a chat with many unplayed voice messages, background the app while transcripts are pending, return, and leave and reopen the chat. Turn one message's transcript off with its side disc. Scroll it out of view and reopen the chat. Confirm it is still hidden, then turn it back on. Turn the setting off and receive another voice message. Repeat with Offline Speech to Text uninstalled, then tap one message's disc. Check light, dark, AMOLED, RTL and 200% text. → **Expected:** The setting is off on a new install and persists across restarts. With it on, each received voice message shows its transcript inside its bubble, under the player and above the time at the trailing corner, as soon as its audio is local and recognition finishes, with no tap; while it works the side disc shows a spinner. Only one recognition runs at a time and transcripts for messages scrolled away do not keep running. Finished transcripts stay after scrolling, backgrounding and reopening the chat. Turning one message off hides only that message and keeps it off while the app runs; turning it back on shows the transcript at once without running recognition again. Your own voice messages are never transcribed. With the setting off, new voice messages need one tap on the disc. Without a provider, automatic mode puts no error under any message and turns their discs off; tapping a disc shows the install guidance. No audio or transcript leaves the device or is sent to the chat.

1. [ ] **MED-058 — Voice send feedback and reply hand-off** — In Settings → Voice & language → Voice notes, turn off Send held voice notes on release. On a slow connection, record a take of a few seconds and tap Send in review. Repeat as a reply to an older message. Then turn on airplane mode, send another voice reply, wait for it to fail, turn airplane mode off and tap Retry on its bubble. Repeat with TalkBack on. Turn on Send held voice notes on release, hold and release a take, then repeat with a slide to cancel and with a tapped, locked recording. → **Expected:** Send changes at once: the arrow becomes a ring inside the same disc and the time reads "Sending", while Play, Discard and Record again are disabled and TalkBack offers no Discard action. Review closes as the bubble appears, for replies too. The pending bubble shows a ring around Play, Play still plays the take, and TalkBack reads its action followed by "Sending"; the delivery clock stays a still icon. A failed voice reply offers Retry and Discard only on its bubble and review stays closed. Retry sends it once, and exactly one voice message arrives on the other device. With the setting on, releasing a held take queues it without a review action. Sliding to cancel sends nothing. Stopping a locked take still opens review.

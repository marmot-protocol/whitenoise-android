1. [ ] **SET-048 — Largest attachments** — Use an account whose chats hold photos, videos, voice messages and files, including files sent with a name. In Settings → Data & storage → Manage storage, open Largest attachments. Read the rows. Tap a photo, a video, a file and a voice message row and close each one. Then tap the check marks of several rows to select them, hold one more row to select it, use Select all and then Clear selection, select two again and tap Remove. Cancel once, then Remove again and confirm. Repeat the selection once with TalkBack, and once in dark theme, RTL and large text. → **Expected:** Every chat's attachments are listed largest first. Photo rows show a thumbnail of the photo and video rows the sender's preview or, once the video was played, its first frame with a small play badge; files and voice messages keep their kind icon. Each row shows the sender's file name as its title on one line, shortened in the middle so the extension stays visible, with its size, received date and chat on one line below, the kind as its icon, and a check mark at the end. An attachment sent without a name shows its kind (Photo, Video, Audio or File) as the title instead. Names never show a folder path or reorder the row in RTL. A selected row takes a tonal fill, TalkBack reports each row as a checkbox and announces the running count and size, and Back clears the selection before leaving the list. Tapping a row opens the attachment without downloading anything: photos and videos in the full-screen viewer, files in the app chosen for them and voice messages in the player, and it never changes the selection. The check mark, or holding a row, toggles selection, and TalkBack reads the check mark as "Select <name>" with its checked state. While anything is selected a pinned bar shows the live count and size ("2 selected · 14 MB") with Remove. The confirmation names the number of attachments and says it frees up to that size; Cancel keeps everything. After confirming, the removed rows leave the list, the selection clears and a message reads "Removed 2 attachments · X freed", and the overview figures drop when you go Back. Labels, sizes and the bar stay readable and unclipped in every theme, RTL and large text.

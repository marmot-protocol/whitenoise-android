1. [ ] **MED-047 — Headphone disconnect pauses playback** — With wired or Bluetooth headphones connected, play a voice message, start read-aloud on a text message, and play a referenced Nostr video. During each, disconnect the headphones. Repeat read-aloud with Speak over playing media enabled while music plays, disconnect while a voice message is still loading, and disconnect while read-aloud loads a seek target outside the queue. Then pause each source yourself and disconnect again. → **Expected:** Each source pauses at once and stays silent on the phone speaker; nothing resumes on its own after the route changes. Read-aloud keeps its paused cursor and resumes from that sentence; a voice message keeps its position; a loading voice message does not start. Disconnecting while read-aloud loads a seek target outside the queue leaves it paused at that target. With nothing playing, disconnecting headphones changes nothing.

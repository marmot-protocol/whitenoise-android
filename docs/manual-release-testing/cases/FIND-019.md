1. [ ] **FIND-019 — Open a global-search result and return** — In a chat with several thousand messages, search a word that matches a message near the start of that history and one in another chat. Scroll the results down, tap the old match, watch the transcript land, then press Back. Tap a second result while the first is still opening, and scroll the transcript yourself while a result is opening. Delete one listed match for everyone from another device, hide another with Delete for me, and let a disappearing match expire, then tap each from the still-open results. Leave the conversation unread before tapping a result in it. Rotate the device while the results and the opened conversation are showing. Repeat with TalkBack, at 200% text and in RTL. → **Expected:** The old match opens centered with its neighbours, briefly highlighted, without scrolling through the history in between, and TalkBack announces "Showing the matching message" once without moving focus. Back returns at once to the same query, filters, loaded results and scroll position with the opened row selected and focused, without the results reloading or flashing a loading state. A second tap or your own scroll wins: the first result never lands, scrolls or highlights afterwards. A deleted, hidden or expired match opens nothing and says "This message is no longer available", never showing its old text. If the message window cannot load, the search stays as it was and the conversation offers "Couldn’t open the message. Retry", which opens the same result. Opening a result marks messages read only as they are seen, as for any other scroll. Rotation keeps the query, filters, results, scroll position and selection.

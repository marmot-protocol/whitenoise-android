[ ] **SET-068 — Declining an invitation with linked devices** — This needs a disposable account with two linked devices, A and B, a second account to send invitations, and a third device C signed in to an account with no other device. Invite the linked account to a group as an ordinary member. On A open the invitation and tap Decline, then Cancel. Tap Decline again and confirm. Invite it to another group and decline on B while A has the invitation open. On C, decline an invitation. Repeat on A in RTL and at the largest font size, with TalkBack. → **Expected:** On A, Decline first asks Decline on all your devices? and explains that the account leaves the chat on every linked device and needs a new invitation to come back. Cancel closes the question and the invitation stays open with Accept and Decline. Confirming declines: A leaves the invitation, and within a minute it is gone from B too, with no Accept or Decline left there. When B declines first, the question on A closes by itself and A no longer shows the invitation. An invitation that makes the account an admin asks the step-down question after this one. On C, which has no other device, Decline declines at once without the question. Interrupt the roster lookup before declining. The invite remains pending and the user can retry. Replace the Welcome for this group while the question is open. Answering the stale question must leave the replacement pending.

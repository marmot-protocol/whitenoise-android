1. [ ] **DIC-017 — Hold the dictation button to dictate and send** — With Dictate on the composer toolbar and the disclosure and microphone permission already accepted, tap Dictate once, then cancel. Next press and hold Dictate, speak a sentence and release. Repeat: hold, speak, slide up past the lock mark and lift your finger, then finish with the ordinary Send, Paste or Cancel control. Repeat: hold, speak, slide toward the text field (left, or right in RTL) until the mark turns into a delete icon and release; also slide that way and back before releasing. Also hold still and pull down the notification shade before lifting. Repeat a hold while the disclosure or permission is still required, in a reply, in light, dark and AMOLED themes, at the largest font size and in RTL, and with TalkBack double-tap. → **Expected:** A tap opens dictation exactly as before. A hold gives a haptic tick, starts dictation and shows a small lock mark above the button that rises with your finger and fills once you are close to locking. Releasing in place stops listening, transcribes and sends the words once, as a reply when replying. Sliding up past the mark locks: dictation keeps listening with its normal bubble and controls after you lift. Sliding toward the text field far enough arms cancel with a second haptic tick; releasing then discards the dictation and sends nothing, while sliding back first and releasing still sends. Pulling down the shade mid-hold never sends; dictation stays open with its normal controls. A hold that needs the disclosure or permission shows that prompt only, never a second tap action. If the speech provider is still being checked when you release, dictation stays open with its normal controls instead of sending. TalkBack double-tap behaves like a tap.

[ ] **DIC-012 — Send and Paste survive a failing final tail** — Dictate one complete sentence, then keep the microphone open over breathing, keyboard or room noise for about a second before choosing Send. Repeat with Paste, and with three to five seconds of the same noise. In controlled runs, make the final caller-audio chunk return no match twice, make it fail with one network or provider-disconnect error and then succeed, stall the provider on a short final chunk, and lose audio focus to another app right after Send. → **Expected:** The recognized sentence sends once (or pastes once) without being left in the composer and without a Not sent warning. An empty final tail gets at most one retry before it is released, and a capture-side silent tail is released without a retry. A first transient fault on the final tail is retried; a short final tail that fails again or stalls is released. A long or unknown-length tail with repeated transient faults, an earlier chunk that keeps failing, or a dictation with no recognized text still keeps the audio and offers recovery. Audio focus loss after Send or Paste does not cancel the chosen action. Stored diagnostics show `caller_audio_tail_dropped` with the fault and tail length for each released tail.

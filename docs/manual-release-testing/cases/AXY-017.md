1. [ ] **AXY-017 — Flip phones and cover screens** — On a flip phone, open a chat on the inner screen, type a draft and scroll up. Half-open the phone like a laptop and stand it on a table, then open and close the keyboard. Open it flat again. Where the phone can run apps on its cover screen, close it with the chat open so White Noise continues on the cover screen, browse the chat list, open a chat and type, then open the phone again. Connect and disconnect a hardware keyboard while a draft is open. → **Expected:** Half open, the messages fill the upright half down to the hinge and the composer sits just below the hinge on the flat half, where the keyboard opens; opening flat returns the composer to the bottom edge. On the cover screen the chat list and the conversation each fill the small screen, the composer and send button stay reachable, and nothing is clipped. Moving between the inner and cover screens, opening or closing the phone and connecting a keyboard keep the open chat, its draft and scroll position without restarting the screen.

1. [ ] **NTF-041 — Pending cards survive opening the app** — Sign in two accounts and make the first one active. With the app closed, receive a message for the second account so its card sits in the shade, and do the same for a conversation of the active account that is not among its eight most recent chats. Open White Noise from the launcher without tapping either card, wait for the chat list to finish loading, then open the share sheet from another app and each card's conversation notification settings from the system. Repeat with message previews turned off in White Noise's notification settings. → **Expected:** Both cards stay in the shade, unchanged, while the app loads and afterwards, and tapping each still opens the right account's conversation at its message. Opening the app never removes a pending card or its conversation notification settings. The system settings keep naming each conversation while previews are on and show the app name instead once previews are off, and the share sheet lists only the active account's recent chats, never the other account's.

1. [ ] **MED-038 — Received-audio transcription** — On a disposable Android 13+ installation, install Offline Speech to Text and prepare a speech model. Receive an Opus voice note and an AAC/M4A recording with distinctive words before and after a pause longer than 15 seconds. Disable automatic audio downloads: confirm Transcribe is disabled until the existing Download action finishes. Open Transcribe, cancel the disclosure once, then confirm. Repeat with Wi-Fi and mobile data off after the recording and model are local. Select and copy transcript text. Cancel during decoding and recognition; close the result, scroll the source off screen, leave the chat, background, lock, rotate and switch accounts while work is pending, then return. Test missing provider/model, denied microphone permissions, an unavailable service, silence/unrecognized audio, malformed files, unsupported PCM output, oversized audio and a timeout; restore the prerequisite and retry. Repeat in light/dark/AMOLED, RTL, 200% text and TalkBack, plus an Android 12 installation. → **Expected:** The disclosure names the external on-device provider and transient, potentially inaccurate text. The entire accepted recording feeds one request and words after the long pause remain represented; no intermediate buffer is treated as the whole recording. The microphone is never recorded and no message is sent or composer draft changed. Only the eligible Offline Speech to Text service receives PCM; there is no system/cloud/microphone fallback. The result is selectable and labeled as generated rather than the sender's text. Close, cancellation, background, source/owner disposal and app lock clear the transcript and cancel work; late callbacks cannot reopen it or enter another account/chat. Failed or incomplete input never produces a successful partial transcript, and unrecognized audio is an error rather than silently discarded speech. Missing provider offers its existing Zapstore listing; permission/model/service failures give guidance and retry. Android 12 reports the Android 13 requirement. Decode admission limits are 512 MiB encoded and decoded audio, 60 minutes of decoded audio, mono/stereo 8–96 kHz PCM16, and 60 seconds of active decoding; recognition has a five-minute deadline and stalled pipe writes a ten-second deadline. A limit refusal is explicit and never truncates the recording. Record exact APK/provider versions and digests; fixture or screenshot tests alone do not prove model recognition or microphone behavior.

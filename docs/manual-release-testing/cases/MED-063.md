[ ] **MED-063 — Share selected photos from Shared Media** — In a chat whose Photos and videos gallery has a message with three photos, another photo message, and a video, open Shared Media → Photos and videos. Long-press a photo of the first message, tap a second photo of that message and the other message's photo, then tap the video. Tap Share and pick a receiving app. Repeat with TalkBack, starting from the Select photo action, and press Back while selecting. Start another share and close the gallery, or switch account, before the share sheet appears. Delete one selected photo from another device while selecting, then tap Share. → **Expected:** Long-press selects the photo and replaces the filters with a count, a cancel button and Share; taps toggle photos, the video stays dimmed and does nothing, and deselecting the last photo or pressing Back ends selection without leaving the gallery. The receiving app gets exactly the three photos under their own names, with no caption or third photo from the first message, and selection ends. TalkBack reads each tile as a checkbox named for its sender, and the count is announced. Closing the gallery or switching account opens no share sheet. A deleted selected photo shares nothing, shows "Couldn’t share photos", deselects it, and the next Share sends the rest.

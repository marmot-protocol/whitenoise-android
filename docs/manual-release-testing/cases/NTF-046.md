1. [ ] **NTF-046 — Message alerts stay quiet while another app plays speech** — In Notifications, confirm "Quiet during podcasts" is on by default. Start a podcast or audiobook in another app (for example AntennaPod) and receive a direct message, a group message, a mention and a group invite from another account; repeat with music playing instead of speech, with the podcast paused and nothing else playing, and with the switch off. Wait at least 10 seconds after any audible alert before the next check, because a message that soon after an alert is silent by design. Restart the app and check the switch keeps its value. Check light/dark/AMOLED, RTL, large text and TalkBack. → **Expected:** With speech playing and the switch on, the direct and group message cards appear without sound or vibration and the podcast keeps playing without a pause. The mention and the invite still alert as their channels allow, and the mention may pause the podcast briefly, as any alert sound does. With music playing, a paused podcast or the switch off, messages alert as their channels allow and music only ducks briefly. The first message after the podcast stops alerts normally. The switch keeps its value across restarts, and blocked, silent and Do Not Disturb settings still apply. Record the device, Android version, player and audio output.

1. [ ] **NTF-046 — Message alerts stay quiet while another app plays speech** — In Notifications, confirm "Quiet during podcasts" is on by default. Start a podcast or audiobook in another app (for example AntennaPod) and receive a direct message, a group message and a mention from another account; repeat with music playing instead of speech, with the podcast paused, and with the switch off. Restart the app and check the switch keeps its value. Check light/dark/AMOLED, RTL, large text and TalkBack. → **Expected:** With speech playing and the switch on, the direct and group message cards appear without sound or vibration and the podcast keeps playing without a pause; the mention still alerts as its channel allows. With music playing, a paused podcast or the switch off, messages alert as their channels allow and music only ducks briefly. The first message after the podcast stops alerts normally. The switch keeps its value across restarts, and blocked, silent and Do Not Disturb settings still apply. Record the device, Android version, player and audio output.

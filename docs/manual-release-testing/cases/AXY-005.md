1. [ ] **AXY-005 — Tablet/foldable/resizable** — Resize across narrow/wide postures where available. On a tablet, an unfolded foldable or a window at least 840dp wide, open Chats, then open a chat, type a draft and scroll up in its history. Tap a second chat, then the first again. Press system Back. Reopen a chat, fold the device or shrink the window to phone width, then unfold or widen it again. Open Settings from the chat list. → **Expected:** Content uses readable width, navigation does not duplicate, and state/selection/draft survive posture changes. At 840dp and wider the chat list stays on the left with the conversation beside it, and the right side shows "Select a chat" until a chat opens. Switching chats replaces the conversation without a blank flash, and the first chat returns with its draft. System Back leaves the app as it does from the chat list instead of closing the conversation. Folding or shrinking keeps the open chat full screen with its draft and scroll position; widening again shows the list beside it. Settings opens in the left side. Hold a phone horizontally (an 840dp-wide or wider window under 480dp tall) with a long chat name, unread badges and a draft, then open and close the keyboard and rotate back. → **Expected:** The chat list and the conversation each take half the width with the divider at the centre, messages start next to the divider without an empty strip for the camera cutout, list rows stay readable and composer controls stay reachable; tablet and unfolded-foldable layouts keep their fixed-width list. Rotation and the keyboard keep the open chat, draft and scroll position. On a book-style foldable, open a chat with the device flat, then hold it half open like a book in both orientations that put the fold upright, and open it flat again. → **Expected:** Half open, the chat list fills one side of the fold and the conversation the other, with nothing drawn across the hinge, including in a right-to-left language; on a foldable narrower than 840dp the list appears beside the conversation only while half open. Opening flat restores the regular layout, keeping the open chat, draft and scroll position.

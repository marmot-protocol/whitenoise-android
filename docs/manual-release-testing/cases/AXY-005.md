1. [ ] **AXY-005 — Tablet/foldable/resizable** — Resize across narrow/wide postures where available. On a tablet, an unfolded foldable or a window at least 840dp wide, open Chats, then open a chat, type a draft and scroll up in its history. Tap a second chat, then the first again. Press system Back. Reopen a chat, fold the device or shrink the window to phone width, then unfold or widen it again. Open Settings from the chat list. → **Expected:** Content uses readable width, navigation does not duplicate, and state/selection/draft survive posture changes. At 840dp and wider the chat list stays on the left with the conversation beside it, and the right side shows "Select a chat" until a chat opens. Switching chats replaces the conversation without a blank flash, and the first chat returns with its draft. System Back leaves the app as it does from the chat list instead of closing the conversation. Folding or shrinking keeps the open chat full screen with its draft and scroll position; widening again shows the list beside it. Settings opens in the left side.

# Dictate text

To use dictation, install a speech service first.

## Set up a speech service

1. Install [Offline Speech to Text](https://zapstore.dev/apps/naddr1qqtkzurs9ehkvenvd9hx2umsv4jkx6r5da6x27r5qyv8wumn8ghj7un9d3shjtn6v9c8xar0wfjjuer9wcpzpys5pkhzxd9dqp4ger8du6p5f6y43tcnzqktjzmwvahq5vumtay4qvzqqqr7pv8t57pf), available on Zapstore.
2. After you install it, open it and follow its setup steps, including any model download.
3. Then go back to White Noise, open **Settings > Voice & language > Dictation**, and choose a service under **Speech provider**.

Which services appear depends on what is installed on your device. If none is found, the app asks you to install and enable one and finish its setup. A keyboard may offer voice typing in its own controls.

## Dictate a message

With Offline Speech to Text, audio is processed on this device. Your device's speech service may send audio to its provider, so check which service you use before you dictate private text.

In a conversation, tap **Dictate text**. It turns your speech into text that you can review and then **Paste** or **Send**, so it never sends a recording. To send audio, hold the voice control instead, as described in [Voice messages](/docs/using/media/android#voice-messages).

## Dictation controls

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| Get Offline Speech to Text | Opens the Zapstore page for an app that transcribes speech on this device. It is not on Google Play. If the page can't open, the app says so. |
| Speech provider | Automatic or a specific provider. Android's selected speech service takes priority. If none is selected, White Noise uses the provider you choose here or, when there is only one engine, that engine. Each provider is labeled **Works inside White Noise**, **Opens provider window**, **Keyboard only** or **In-app support not verified**. Choosing or cancelling records no audio and leaves your draft unchanged. After you choose one, tap dictation again to start. |
| Pause other audio | Pauses music and podcasts while you dictate and resumes them afterwards. White Noise speech and voice messages always pause. |
| Finish dictation | **When I choose Paste or Send**, or after a number of seconds of silence that you pick from the list. |
| When finished | Applies when dictation ends by silence. **Paste into draft** lets you review or edit the text first. **Send message** sends only if the chat and the draft are unchanged. |
| Open Android Settings | Opens your device's voice input settings, where you manage speech services and keyboard voice typing. If it can't open, the app says so. |

These settings apply on this device only. If sending is blocked, recognized text returns to the draft, so review it before you send again.

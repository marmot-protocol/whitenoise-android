# Send a message

Both people need White Noise, or another compatible Marmot client, set up and connected. A Nostr public key (an address starting with `npub`) identifies the person. Their app also needs to publish invitation keys so you can start a chat. If that fails, see [Invitation problems](/docs/troubleshooting/invitations).

Find the person using their Nostr public key or profile QR code, then start a direct chat.

## Find the person

1. On **Chats**, tap the pencil control labelled **New Message** to open the recipient search.
2. In **Name or npub**, type a name or Nostr address, or tap **Paste** to insert a copied npub or profile link. To scan a profile code instead, tap **Connect with QR**.
3. Choose the person from the results. Results are grouped as **People in chats**, **Following**, **Network results** and **On this device**, and people you follow are marked **You follow**.

The same screen also has **Invite a friend** and **Talk to your agent** rows, and a **New Group** row for group chats.

If White Noise marks an address as unverified, tap **Retry** or confirm the address with the person before you message them. If the network search fails or looks incomplete, check your connection and tap **Retry**; pasting their npub directly also works.

## Send your first message

1. Open the chat and wait while White Noise sets up encryption and shows an empty conversation.
2. Type your message in the composer and send it.

A message can hold up to 8,000 characters. A longer one is not sent, and White Noise shows **Message too long. Shorten your message, then send again.**

**Expected result:** your text appears in the chat. To check it, select the message and tap **Info** to open **Message Details**, where **Status** shows **Sending…**, **Sent** or **Failed**. The list of people under it repeats that one status for each member, not a separate receipt per person. **Sent** does not tell you that anyone received or read the message, and they may be offline. **Received** appears only on messages sent to you.

If starting the chat fails, check your connection and tap **Retry**. If White Noise shows **Chat created** with **Open chat**, the chat exists and is still reaching your list, so wait a moment and tap **Open chat**. If a message of yours fails, White Noise shows **Send failed**. Check your connection, then choose **Retry** on the message, or **Discard failed message** to remove it from your chat. Some failed sends offer only **Discard**. See [Delivery problems](/docs/troubleshooting/delivery).

## Let others message you

In **Settings**, tap your profile card (**Share & Connect**) to show your QR code or copy your npub for others.

## Invite someone new

Tap **Invite a friend** to open your device's sharing options with a link to download White Noise.

## Accept or decline an invitation

When someone starts a chat or group with you, it appears as an invitation in your chat list. Open it and tap **Accept** to join or **Decline** to turn it down. If the invitation is still being checked, wait for it to finish, and if checking fails, tap **Retry**. See [Invitation problems](/docs/troubleshooting/invitations) for more help.
